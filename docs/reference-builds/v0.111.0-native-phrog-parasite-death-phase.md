# v0.111.0 native Phrog Parasite elite death phase

Pinned source:
`Flaaax/STS2-source@706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20`.

- `Core/Models/Monsters/PhrogParasite.cs: AfterAddedToRoom`
  applies **4 Infested** power (single-stack type).
- `Core/Models/Powers/InfestedPower.cs: AfterDeath` fires
  when the owner dies and its death is not prevented. It invokes
  `CreatureCmd.Add` four times in source order, creating Wrigglers
  in `wriggler1` through `wriggler4` and marking each
  `Wriggler.StartStunned = true` **before** adding them.
  Infested prevents the boss encounter from ending before the
  death transition has been processed.
- `Core/Models/Monsters/Wriggler.cs: GenerateMoveStateMachine`
  starts stunned Wrigglers in an actual `SPAWNED_MOVE` with
  `StunIntent`, no effects, and a follow-up to the
  `INIT_MOVE` slot-dependent selector: odd slots Bite and
  even slots Wriggle, then these two moves alternate.
  The source does not represent the stun as a missing intent or a
  permanently skipped turn.
- Each monster receives a newly assigned native CombatId and
  an independent monster-local native RNG seeded from the room
  coordinate and its ID. Its initial HP consumes one persistent
  `run.niche` draw with the pinned unique-HP exclusion set,
  including the dying parasite while its hook is active.

## Implemented

- The **experimental native PhrogParasiteElite** entry now has
  source-correct Infested power amount **4**, without affecting
  the default prototype's existing amount **1**.
- The four death-triggered Wrigglers receive an explicit
  `spawned` no-op move and `spawned_move → init` AI state.
  Their first move is a **precommitted, visible Spawned/Stun**,
  rather than a silent artificial skipped enemy action.
  Their next move follows their respective native encounter slot.
- Their created models and HP continue using the existing
  `NativeCombatRngEntryV111.AppendSummonedEnemy` and its persistent
  native RNG cursor/CombatId allocation. The original dead
  parasite retains its historical combat ID and RNG context.
- The added Wriggler move state is not reachable in ordinary
  legacy prototype combat, which still uses its established
  skip-one-action approximation. Unsupported native content
  and other death hooks remain gated.
- The exact enemy-turn-start poison-death ordering relative to
  the native initiative snapshot is not yet audited. In native
  opt-in mode that specific entry `enemyActionSkips: 2`
  raises `NotSupportedException` rather than pretending source
  timing parity. Player-turn deaths are supported.

## Tests

The focused `NativePhrogParasiteDeathPhaseV111Tests` suite:
- checks native initial Infested x4 and 48 independent deaths each
  at A0, A8 and A10 (144 total seeded death oracles);
- independently reproduces all four `run.niche` draws,
  HP exclusion/order, native CombatIds, and per-monster contexts;
- tests snapshot immutability, idempotent death-hook resolution,
  immutable forks and strict unsupported poison-start timing;
- runs an actual native opt-in map-entry combat through a
  lethal player card, observed Spawned/Stun intent, the next
  Wriggler turn, and replay from an identical snapshot.

This is source-audited **combat-hook** coverage, not full
game-oracle differential parity. Rewards, native encounter bag
selection, live boss music/visual effects, and other boss phases
remain prototype-backed.
