# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 111 — Kusarigama

Kusarigama is an Uncommon shared relic. Every third Attack played by its owner
in a turn deals 6 unpowered damage to a random living enemy. The counter resets
at the next player turn; melted copies do not count or deal damage.

The executable fixture sends three owner Attack play events, checks that only
the third deals damage, advances to a new turn, and checks the next third
Attack. It also confirms melted Kusarigama is inactive.
