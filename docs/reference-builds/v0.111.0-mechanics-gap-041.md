## Phase-aware Wriggler stun

The important unresolved fidelity boundary was the first-turn stun after Phrog dies.

The old representation used a single boolean `SkipNextEnemyAction`. That was too coarse because a
death during enemy-turn status resolution must survive one additional enemy-action pass compared with
a death during the player turn.

Enemy combat state now carries an integer `EnemyActionSkipsRemaining`.

Death-summon resolution receives the number of future enemy-action passes to skip:

- player-turn death: 1;
- enemy-turn-start death such as Poison: 2, because the newly spawned Wrigglers first skip the
  current enemy-action pass and must still be Stunned on the following player turn/enemy phase;
- death after the current enemy-action pass: 1.

The legacy boolean remains readable for older content, but new death-summon timing uses the explicit
counter.

Focused regressions verify both native timing shapes.

### Player-turn kill

After killing Phrog with an Attack:

1. four Wrigglers spawn immediately;
2. the upcoming enemy turn is skipped;
3. on the following enemy turn, two use Nasty Bite and two use Wriggle.

### Enemy-turn-start kill

If Poison kills Phrog at enemy-turn start:

1. four Wrigglers spawn before that enemy-action pass;
2. they skip that current pass;
3. they remain Stunned through the next player turn and skip the following enemy-action pass;
4. they begin their Bite/Wriggle pattern on the subsequent turn.

## Wriggler pattern

Pinned values:

- HP 17–21 at A0–A7;
- HP 18–22 at A8+;
- Nasty Bite: 6 damage, 7 at A9+;
- Wriggle: add 1 Infection and gain 2 Strength.

The four death summons use alternating semantic opener slots:

- positions 0 and 2: Bite first;
- positions 1 and 3: Wriggle first.

After the opener each Wriggler alternates between the two moves.

## Elite selector

Overgrowth's elite inventory is:

- `BYGONE_EFFIGY_ELITE`;
- `BYRDONIS_ELITE`;
- `PHROG_PARASITE_ELITE`.

The run state already stores a forkable remaining-elite bag.

Selection is equal-odds from the remaining bag. When the bag empties, it refills from all three
elites. The immediate-repeat restriction is defined over **elite encounters**, not over the raw
global encounter history.

This pass fixes the selector to look backward for the most recent prior elite encounter, so an
ordinary fight or other room between two elite rooms cannot accidentally permit the same elite twice
in succession.

As a result:

- the first three elite encounters are all distinct;
- later elites may repeat;
- the same elite is never selected on two consecutive elite draws.

Exact native RNG encoding/call order remains outside the current claim.
