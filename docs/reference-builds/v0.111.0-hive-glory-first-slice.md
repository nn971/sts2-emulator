# Pinned v0.111.0 Hive / Glory first implementation slice

Source: `Flaaax/STS2-source@706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20`.
Target build: `41cef1ea`; emulator version remains v0.111.0.
This note records **source-audited executable fragments**, not native replay fidelity.

## Native later-act inventory

- Hive is the second act (native `Index = 1`, `BaseNumberOfRooms = 14`)
  with **20** ordinary encounters (4 weak, 10 normal, 3 elite, 3 boss),
  **10** regional events and 3 Ancient candidates.
- Glory is the third act (native `Index = 2`, `BaseNumberOfRooms = 13`)
  with **18** ordinary encounters (3 weak, 9 normal, 3 elite, 3 boss),
  **7** regional events and 3 Ancient candidates.
- `PrototypeNativeLaterActs.HiveEncounterNames` and
  `.GloryEncounterNames` preserve the exact `GenerateAllEncounters`
  names in the native act source. They are **catalogue-only** lists:
  an identifier does not certify the encounter's logic is implemented.

## Executable slice

- **Hive / Bugslayer**: `Bugslayer.cs`, `Exterminate.cs`, `Squash.cs`.
  The two options each grant a specific card. Exterminate costs 1 and
  deals 3(4) damage **four times to each enemy**. Squash costs 1 and
  deals 10(12) damage, then applies 2(3) Vulnerable to its single target.
  The resulting cards are marked Event-only, outside Silent random drops.
- **Hive worker monsters (isolated models)**:
  `BowlbugEgg.cs` (21–22 HP, A8 23–24; repeat Bite 7/8 followed by
  self Block 7/8) and `BowlbugNectar.cs` (35–38 HP, A8 36–39;
  Thrash 3, Strength +15/+16, then Thrash 3 forever).
  The next batch supplies BowlbugRock and its full weak formation,
  including the Imbalanced fully blocked attack response.
- **Glory worker monsters**:
  `DevotedSculptor.cs` (162 HP, A8 172; Ritual +9 once, Savage 12/15
  repeatedly) and `TurretOperator.cs` (41 HP, A8 51; 3/4 × 5 twice,
  then Strength +1, loop).
  `DevotedSculptorWeak.cs` is registered as a one-monster encounter
  for deterministic forced combat tests; `TurretOperatorWeak.cs`
  cannot yet be implemented without the LivingShield and Rampart power.

## Hive weak encounters (next source-backed batch)

- `BowlbugsWeak.cs`: a fixed `BowlbugRock` in the native `odd`
  slot, plus a separately rolled `BowlbugEgg` or `BowlbugNectar`
  in `even`. Selection is by the existing deterministic combat RNG
  and persists as a concrete formation before combat begins.
- `BowlbugRock.cs` / `ImbalancedPower.cs`: HP 45–48 (A8+ 46–49),
  Headbutt 15 (A9+ 16); when a Headbutt is completely blocked,
  Rock enters an off-balance condition and its next intent is Dizzy,
  after which it recovers and returns to Headbutt. The flag is part of
  immutable combat state and the intent is committed at turn boundaries,
  not on observation. An unblocked hit does not activate the condition.
- `BowlbugSilk.cs`: HP 40–43 (A8+ 41–44), opening Toxic Spit
  (Weak 1) followed by alternating Thrash (4×2, A9+ 5×2) and
  Toxic Spit. It is registered for future BowlbugsNormal, not silently
  included in the two-member BowlbugsWeak encounter.
- `ExoskeletonsWeak.cs`: exactly three Exoskeletons in slots
  `first`, `second`, `third`, with opening Skitter, Mandibles,
  and Enrage respectively. Subsequent moves follow the native
  non-repeating random branch. HP 24–28 (A8+ 26–30);
  Skitter 1×3 (A9+ ×4), Mandibles 8 (A9+ 9), Enrage Strength +2.
- `HardToKillPower.cs`: each Exoskeleton starts with Hard to Kill 9,
  capping each *individual* unblocked HP-loss hit at 9. Four separate
  100-damage hits are needed to defeat a 28-HP Exoskeleton, because
  the cap is not consumed or shared over an entire turn.

- `TunnelerWeak.cs` / `Tunneler.cs` / `BurrowedPower.cs`:
  one 87-HP Tunneler (A8+ 92); Bite 13 (A9+ 15), then Burrow
  (persist 32 Block, A8+ 37), then Below 23 (A9+ 26) repeatedly.
  Burrowed vetoes automatic enemy Block clearing. When damage breaks
  all remaining Block, the power is removed and the *already announced*
  Below is replaced by a one-turn Dizzy, followed by Bite. Partial
  Block loss does not interrupt the enemy. Its power/intent state is
  fork-safe and deterministic.

`ThievingHopperWeak` remains explicitly unimplemented:
stealing a real combat deck card and restoring it after escape still
requires dedicated physical-card/theft behavior. The native Hive map
and encounter bags remain disabled; all three new weak encounters
have weight zero in the legacy selector.
The pinned source establishes the mechanics above but no live reference
run/parity corpus is currently available.

## Integration/availability policy

All entries live in typed registries, with combat effects resolved by
the existing kernel. **They are not yet eligible in random routes.**
The Hive Bugslayer event and Glory Sculptor encounter have `Weight = 0`
while native Act 2/3 map/event/encounter generators are unfinished.
Configured `Overgrowth/Underdocks → Hive → Glory` runs still reject
an attempted native act transition instead of reverting to prototype
Hive/Glory opponents. Explicit testing constructs may inject these
model IDs, and their RNG is still the emulator's prototype implementation.

Do not mark Hive/Glory native runs complete until their pool lists,
weak encounter cycling, rules, ancient entry, all unsupported content,
and transitions are implemented. No live v111 oracle traces are
available; postpone replay-fidelity claims until the user again has a
means to capture them.
