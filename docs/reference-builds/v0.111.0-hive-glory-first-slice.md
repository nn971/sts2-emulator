# Pinned v0.111.0 Hive / Glory first implementation slice

Source: `Flaaax/STS2-source@706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20`.
Target build: `41cef1ea`; emulator version remains v0.111.0.
This note records **source-audited executable fragments**, not native replay fidelity.

## Native later-act inventory

- Hive is the second act (native `Index = 1`, `BaseNumberOfRooms = 14`)
  with **20** ordinary encounters (4 weak, 10 normal, 3 elite, 3 boss),
  **10** regional events and 3 Ancient candidates.
- Glory is the third act (native `Index = 2`, `BaseNumberOfRooms = 13`)
  with **18** ordinary encounters (3 weak, 9 normal, 3 elite, 3 boss),
  **7** regional events and 3 Ancient candidates.
- `PrototypeNativeLaterActs.HiveEncounterNames` and
  `.GloryEncounterNames` preserve the exact `GenerateAllEncounters`
  names in the native act source. They are **catalogue-only** lists:
  an identifier does not certify the encounter's logic is implemented.

## Executable slice

- **Hive / Bugslayer**: `Bugslayer.cs`, `Exterminate.cs`, `Squash.cs`.
  The two options each grant a specific card. Exterminate costs 1 and
  deals 3(4) damage **four times to each enemy**. Squash costs 1 and
  deals 10(12) damage, then applies 2(3) Vulnerable to its single target.
  The resulting cards are marked Event-only, outside Silent random drops.
- **Hive worker monsters (isolated models)**:
  `BowlbugEgg.cs` (21–22 HP, A8 23–24; repeat Bite 7/8 followed by
  self Block 7/8) and `BowlbugNectar.cs` (35–38 HP, A8 36–39;
  Thrash 3, Strength +15/+16, then Thrash 3 forever).
  The next batch supplies BowlbugRock and its full weak formation,
  including the Imbalanced fully blocked attack response.
- **Glory worker monsters**:
  `DevotedSculptor.cs` (162 HP, A8 172; Ritual +9 once, Savage 12/15
  repeatedly) and `TurretOperator.cs` (41 HP, A8 51; 3/4 × 5 twice,
  then Strength +1, loop).
  `DevotedSculptorWeak.cs` is registered as a one-monster encounter
  for deterministic forced combat tests; `TurretOperatorWeak.cs`
  cannot yet be implemented without the LivingShield and Rampart power.

## Hive weak encounters (next source-backed batch)

- `BowlbugsWeak.cs`: a fixed `BowlbugRock` in the native `odd`
  slot, plus a separately rolled `BowlbugEgg` or `BowlbugNectar`
  in `even`. Selection is by the existing deterministic combat RNG
  and persists as a concrete formation before combat begins.
- `BowlbugRock.cs` / `ImbalancedPower.cs`: HP 45–48 (A8+ 46–49),
  Headbutt 15 (A9+ 16); when a Headbutt is completely blocked,
  Rock enters an off-balance condition and its next intent is Dizzy,
  after which it recovers and returns to Headbutt. The flag is part of
  immutable combat state and the intent is committed at turn boundaries,
  not on observation. An unblocked hit does not activate the condition.
- `BowlbugSilk.cs`: HP 40–43 (A8+ 41–44), opening Toxic Spit
  (Weak 1) followed by alternating Thrash (4×2, A9+ 5×2) and
  Toxic Spit. It is registered for future BowlbugsNormal, not silently
  included in the two-member BowlbugsWeak encounter.
- `ExoskeletonsWeak.cs`: exactly three Exoskeletons in slots
  `first`, `second`, `third`, with opening Skitter, Mandibles,
  and Enrage respectively. Subsequent moves follow the native
  non-repeating random branch. HP 24–28 (A8+ 26–30);
  Skitter 1×3 (A9+ ×4), Mandibles 8 (A9+ 9), Enrage Strength +2.
- `HardToKillPower.cs`: each Exoskeleton starts with Hard to Kill 9,
  capping each *individual* unblocked HP-loss hit at 9. Four separate
  100-damage hits are needed to defeat a 28-HP Exoskeleton, because
  the cap is not consumed or shared over an entire turn.

- `TunnelerWeak.cs` / `Tunneler.cs` / `BurrowedPower.cs`:
  one 87-HP Tunneler (A8+ 92); Bite 13 (A9+ 15), then Burrow
  (persist 32 Block, A8+ 37), then Below 23 (A9+ 26) repeatedly.
  Burrowed vetoes automatic enemy Block clearing. When damage breaks
  all remaining Block, the power is removed and the *already announced*
  Below is replaced by a one-turn Dizzy, followed by Bite. Partial
  Block loss does not interrupt the enemy. Its power/intent state is
  fork-safe and deterministic.

- `ThievingHopperWeak.cs` / `ThievingHopper.cs` / `SwipePower.cs`:
  one 79 HP (A8+ 84) Hopper. Initial 5-turn Escape Artist display timer
  and move chain Thievery (17 / A9+ 19), Flutter, Hat Trick (21 / 23),
  Nab (14 / 16), Escape. Theft is resolved BEFORE the first hit:
  select one persistent card currently in draw/discard, prioritizing
  Uncommon, then Common/Rare/Event, then Basic/Quest, then Ancient.
  The chosen card is removed from combat and the player's persistent
  deck, while an instanced Swipe carries the exact card instance,
  upgrade level and state. Killing the thief restores the original
  deck card once and exposes its native special-return reward; letting
  the thief escape permanently loses that card. If no eligible deck
  card is in draw/discard, Thievery still attacks but does not steal.
- `FlutterPower.cs`: gives five charges of 50% damage reduction
  against **powered attacks only**, decremented when those hits deal
  unblocked damage. Depleting all five interrupts the announced move
  for one stunned turn, then resumes the original progression.
  A non-powered hit does not change charges.

All **four** native Hive weak encounter models are now represented with
`Weight = 0` in the legacy selector, and are available only in
explicitly forced/synthetic fights until the native Act 2 map and
encounter bag are implemented. Important boundaries: native
`CombatCardGeneration` RNG is approximated with the emulator's
combat stream; source-level Imbued enchantment priority is not modeled
in the current single-player card-enchantment registry; damage rounding
and the exact native post-theft special reward presentation have no
live-oracle validation. The pinned source establishes the core
mechanics above, but no real reference run/parity corpus is available.

## Hive normal encounter batch (source-backed, gated)

These four encounters are registered with `Weight = 0` pending a
complete native Hive encounter bag and Act 2 map. No fabricated
later-act progression is enabled.

- `BowlbugsNormal.cs`: one fixed Rock in `first` and **two distinct**
  workers chosen without replacement from Egg, Silk, Nectar in
  `middle` and `last`. The source dictionary's selection order is
  preserved. Both selected workers retain independent enemy instance
  IDs, turn states, and future intent.
- `ExoskeletonsNormal.cs`: four Exoskeletons in exact named slots
  `first`, `second`, `third`, `fourth`. The fourth starts with
  `RAND`, choosing Skitter or Mandibles, unlike the initial three
  slots' fixed Skitter / Mandibles / Enrage sequence. The typed
  Conditional AI graph now allows a branch to **another AI state**
  (Random here), rather than demanding a directly targeted move.
  The roll happens when intent is committed, not in public observation.
- `ChompersNormal.cs` / `Chomper.cs`: two independently targetable
  Chompers, each initially holding Artifact 2. HP 60–64, A8+ 63–67.
  Clamp deals 8×2 (A9+ 9×2); Screech creates 3 temporary Dazed in
  discard. One Chomper begins with Clamp; the other has
  `ScreamFirst=true` and begins with Screech. Internal slot labels
  `clamp-first` / `screech-first` store this instance-local flag;
  these are emulator bookkeeping names rather than original Godot
  encounter slot strings.
- `MytesNormal.cs` / `Myte.cs` / `Toxic.cs`: two Mytes with native
  `first` and `second` slots. HP 61–67 (A8+ 64–69).
  First opens Toxic (create two temporary Toxic in hand);
  second opens Suck (4 damage and Strength 2; A9+ 6/3).
  Subsequent move loop is Toxic → Bite 13 (A9+ 15) → Suck.
  Toxic costs 1, Exhausts on play and inflicts 5 blockable damage
  when left in hand at player turn end. Toxic is unupgradable and
  unavailable in ordinary rewards. Generated hand cards never gain a
  persistent deck identity; the emulator sends hand-overflow cards to
  discard when at its 10-card capacity, a documented approximation
  until native overflow behavior is checked.

All four encounters are checked with deterministic formation sampling,
instance-specific first intents, turn-by-turn attack/status outcomes,
fork equivalence and a reviewed executable-coverage baseline. Their
RNG still uses emulator streams; source-backed unit tests do not
establish live-game replay parity.

## Hive reactive normal encounters: Spiny Toad and Slumbering Beetle

Native source: `SpinyToadNormal.cs`, `SpinyToad.cs`,
`SlumberingBeetleNormal.cs`, `SlumberingBeetle.cs`,
`ThornsPower.cs`, `SlumberPower.cs`, `PlatingPower.cs`
from the pinned `Flaaax/STS2-source` commit.

- **Spiny Toad Normal:** a single Spiny Toad (HP 116–119;
  A8+ 121–124). It repeats Protruding Spikes (Thorns +5),
  Spike Explosion (23 damage, A9+ 25; then Thorns −5),
  and Tongue Lash (17 damage, A9+ 19). The existing power
  application and player-card attack retaliation pathways implement
  Thorns before each powered hit, including while the attacker is
  fully blocked. The negative application removes exactly the
  five Thorns stacks after the explosion attack.
- **Slumbering Beetle Normal:** fixed Rock at `first`, Silk at
  `second`, and Slumbering Beetle at `third`. Beetle starts
  with HP 86 (A8+ 89), Plating 15 (A8+ 18) and Slumber 3.
  Snore repeats until the enemy-side sleep countdown expires;
  then the protective Plating is removed and Roll Out attacks
  for 16 (A9+ 18) and gains Strength 2 on each action.
  An early *unblocked damage* hit removes Slumber and Plating,
  interrupts the already committed sleeping intent for one
  stunned action, and resumes with Roll Out. Fully blocked hits
  never wake it. Conditional AI now supports a power-present
  versus power-absent predicate at commitment time.
- The native Slumber hook is damage-agnostic; the emulator's
  reused wake-on-HP-loss hook is presently exercised for combat
  attack/normal hit paths. Other unusual unblocked damage sources
  (e.g. certain status callbacks) need subsequent audited tests.
  The source lacks a live oracle trace, and cross-build RNG parity
  is not established.

Both encounters have legacy selector `Weight=0`, and the native
Hive room/event/encounter pools and Act 2 map transitions remain
disabled until sufficiently complete. The entries are available
to deterministic forced-combat tests only.

## Hive normal encounters: Hunter Killer and Louse Progenitor

Pinned sources: `HunterKillerNormal.cs`, `HunterKiller.cs`,
`TenderPower.cs`, `LouseProgenitorNormal.cs`,
`LouseProgenitor.cs`, `CurlUpPower.cs`.

- **Hunter Killer Normal**: one 121 HP Hunter Killer (A8+ 126).
  Goop applies Tender 1 first, then the enemy chooses Bite 17/19
  (cannot repeat directly) or Puncture 7/8 × 3 (relative weight 2);
  choices are committed with the combat RNG. Tender reduces both
  Strength and Dexterity by 1 at each completed player card, then
  restores exactly the amounts applied at player-side turn end.
  The power stores its temporary reductions per instance, allowing
  artifact-prevented debuffs and state forks to behave independently.
- **Louse Progenitor Normal**: one HP 134–136 (A8+ 138–141),
  beginning with Curl Up 14 (A8+ 18). The fixed Web 9/10 plus
  Frail 2, Curl (Block 14/18, Strength 5/7), Pounce 14/16
  cycle preserves native follow-up order. The initial Curl Up
  observes the first powered card attack, including fully blocked
  hits, and gives its Block *after the entire card finishes*,
  consuming the power once. A replayed or multi-hit card cannot
  trigger Curl Up twice.

Both encounters are force-test-only and excluded from native Hive
map/encounter selection. Enemy sprite curl state and unusual card
source attribution beyond conventional powered card attacks remain
provisional. Real-run parity and native combat RNG call ordering
are awaiting oracle evidence.

## Hive summoning normals: Ovicopter and The Obscura

Pinned native references: `OvicopterNormal.cs`, `Ovicopter.cs`,
`ToughEgg.cs`, `HatchPower.cs`, `TheObscuraNormal.cs`,
`TheObscura.cs`, `Parafright.cs`, `IllusionPower.cs`,
`MinionPower.cs` at the audited v111 source commit.

- **Ovicopter Normal:** one boss-like normal in the native `ovicopter`
  slot, HP 124–130 (A8+ 126–132). Initial Lay Eggs creates up to
  three independently targetable Tough Eggs in *reverse* slot order
  `egg5`, `egg4`, `egg3`. Slots occupied by defeated eggs
  remain consumed, matching native `Enemies.All(c => c.SlotName != s)`.
  The parent follows Smash (16, A9+17) and Tenderizer (7/8 plus
  Vulnerable 2), then uses Lay when at most three teammates including
  itself remain alive, otherwise Nutritional Paste (Strength 3/4).
  This decision is committed once, at the enemy-intent boundary.
  Eggs have 14–18 HP (A8+ 15–19) and Hatch 2 when summoned during
  the enemy side; they transform **in place** to a Hatchling with
  19–22 HP (A8+ 20–23) and recurring Nibble (4/5), preserving
  instance ID and minion status.
- **The Obscura Normal:** one original in `obscura`, HP 123
  (A8+129), initially summons a Parafright in `illusion` with
  21 HP. Subsequent intent is a nonrepeating RNG choice among
  Piercing Gaze (10/11), Wail (+3 Strength to all live teammates)
  and Hardening Strike (6/7 attack and Block). Parafright Slam
  deals 16/17. Illusion/Minion powers keep the same instance
  in a dead, untargetable, pending-revival state and restore it on
  a later enemy side-turn while the original lives. Defeating
  the last primary ends the fight even when secondary minions live.

All entries remain `Weight=0` and available only by deterministic
forced-combat construction. The emulator represents post-hatch Tough Egg
with an explicit `hatchling` species-model ID, while native keeps
the ToughEgg type and changes its title and moves. Native per-monster
`Niche` RNG (egg skin, hatch HP) is still mapped onto the emulator's
combat stream; precise call order, repeated Illusion with an occupied
slot and revival/death visual timing lack real-game replay fixtures.

## Hive elite first slice (not fidelity-complete)

Pinned native references: `DecimillipedeElite.cs`,
`DecimillipedeSegment.cs`, `ReattachPower.cs`,
`EntomancerElite.cs`, `Entomancer.cs`, `PersonalHivePower.cs`,
`InfestedPrismsElite.cs`, `InfestedPrism.cs`, `VitalSparkPower.cs`.

The three elite encounter IDs are registered at **Weight=0** as
forced-test inventory. This first pass implements HP/Ascension tables
and scripted move payloads. Entomancer: BEES 3×7 (A9+ 3×8),
SPEAR 18/20, Pheromone Spit's initial +1 Strength.
Prism: JAB 15/17, RADIATE 11/13 and equal Block,
WHIRLWIND 5/6 ×3, PULSATE 8/10 plus Block 20/22 and
Vital Spark +2/+3. Three Decimillipede segment models:
40–46 HP (A8+ 46–52), Writhe 5/6 ×2,
Bulk 6/7 +2 Strength and Constrict 8/9 +1 Weak.

**Unsupported marks:** Entomancer's PersonalHive creates Dazed
on powered hits and caps/increments itself conditional on Pheromone
Spit; Prism's VitalSpark taints Skill cards and applies TaintedPower
on play; Decimillipede performs staggered rotational opening moves,
distinct even HP assignments, shared lethal/death state and reattachment
healing. These must not be interpreted as implemented by an empty
registered power. None of these elites is enabled in automatic Hive
routing or asserted game-play parity.

## Integration/availability policy

All entries live in typed registries, with combat effects resolved by
the existing kernel. **They are not yet eligible in random routes.**
The Hive Bugslayer event and Glory Sculptor encounter have `Weight = 0`
while native Act 2/3 map/event/encounter generators are unfinished.
Configured `Overgrowth/Underdocks → Hive → Glory` runs still reject
an attempted native act transition instead of reverting to prototype
Hive/Glory opponents. Explicit testing constructs may inject these
model IDs, and their RNG is still the emulator's prototype implementation.

Do not mark Hive/Glory native runs complete until their pool lists,
weak encounter cycling, rules, ancient entry, all unsupported content,
and transitions are implemented. No live v111 oracle traces are
available; postpone replay-fidelity claims until the user again has a
means to capture them.
