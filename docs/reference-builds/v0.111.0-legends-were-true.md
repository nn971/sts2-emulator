## Implemented route

- The event is eligible in Act 1 when the deck is nonempty and current HP is
  at least 10. It is registered in both Overgrowth and Underdocks.
- **Nab the map** adds the unplayable Spoils Map Quest card. Its saved target
  is Act 2. When the Hive map is generated, the emulator assigns the card to
  its first Treasure node. Entering that node grants 600 gold and removes the
  card from the deck.
- **Slowly find an exit** loses 8 HP, then offers one random potion through
  the event reward continuation. The player may take, replace, or skip it.

## Remaining differences

- The map stores the quest assignment on the Treasure node, but does not
  project the native visible quest marker or quest UI.
- Treasure-node selection follows the emulator's generated-node ordering;
  native `GetAllMapPoints` traversal and map-generation call order have not
  been matched.
- The potion is drawn from the prototype potion pool and RNG stream. Native
  unlocked character/shared potion filtering and reward RNG parity remain
  unverified.
