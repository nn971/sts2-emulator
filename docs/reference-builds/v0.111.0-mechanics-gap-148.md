# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 148 — Rainbow Ring

The pinned Rainbow Ring resets its Attack, Skill and Power counters at the
owner's side-turn start. Once the owner has played at least one of each card
type during that turn, it grants 1 Strength and 1 Dexterity. It can activate
only once per turn and is a Rare shared relic.

The emulator adds the relic to its shared Rare pool. Combat state now counts
Power cards alongside Attacks and Skills; a generic relic condition checks that
all three counts are positive and caps the trigger at once each turn. Its
strength and dexterity effects resolve on the completed card-play event. The
prototype does not model the relic's UI activation status or visual effects,
and no new executable coverage claim is recorded.
