# v0.111.0 mechanics gap — pass 039

This pass replaces the transitional post-weak selector with the typed **Overgrowth normal encounter
bag**.

## Normal bag

The Overgrowth normal inventory contains twelve encounters. After the first three ordinary combats
consume three distinct weak encounters, subsequent ordinary combats draw from the normal inventory
without replacement.

Run state now stores both:

- remaining weak encounter IDs;
- remaining normal encounter IDs;
- the total number of ordinary combats started.

Both arrays deep-clone through Fork(), so sibling search branches can consume different encounter
histories safely.

## Adjacency exclusions

Before drawing from the remaining normal bag, the selector applies the source-backed adjacency
restrictions when another candidate remains. These include:

- Fuzzy Wurm Crawler and Overgrowth Crawlers;
- Shrinker Beetle and Overgrowth Crawlers;
- the slime-family encounters: weak Slimes, Shroom and Slime, and Swarm Slimes;
- Shroom and Slime versus Overgrowth Flora.

If all remaining candidates would violate an adjacency restriction, the bag remains usable rather
than deadlocking.

## Invariants

The Act-1 pool validator now distinguishes the three-combat weak phase from the continuing ordinary
combat count.

It validates:

- weak remaining-count consistency using min(3, ordinary combats);
- normal remaining-count consistency after combat 3;
- uniqueness and membership of both bags;
- deep-fork safety;
- weight-0 native Overgrowth encounters as promoted selectable content.

The previous invariant that capped ordinary combats at three is removed.

## Regressions

Focused tests verify:

- new runs initialize both bags;
- the first three ordinary fights remain weak and distinct;
- the fourth fight consumes one normal encounter without changing the remaining weak entry;
- successive normal fights are distinct and shrink the normal bag;
- normal bag state deep-clones across forks;
- the full 500-seed sweep terminates without invariant violations.

## Milestone

The **ordinary-combat layer of Overgrowth is now complete at the current semantic fidelity level**:

- 4 weak encounters;
- 12 normal encounters;
- source-backed monster mechanics and encounter compositions;
- weak and normal no-replacement selection state;
- known adjacency exclusions.

Oracle differential coverage and exact native RNG parity remain separate later passes.
