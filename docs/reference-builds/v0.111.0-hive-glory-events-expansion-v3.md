## New Hive event mechanics

**Spirit Grafter**: Let It In heals 25 and adds Metamorphosis.
Rejection presents a player-selected eligible persistent deck card
to upgrade; its *10 unblockable HP loss is deferred until after the
selection* (or takes effect immediately when no valid candidates
remain). Card selection uses the existing serialized continuation.

**Zen Weaver**: source eligibility requires at least 125 Gold upon
event selection, independent of card removal availability. Breathing
Techniques spends 50 Gold for two Enlightenments; Emotional
Awareness spends 125 to remove one player-selected card; Arachnid
Acupuncture spends 250 to remove two different player-selected
cards. The 250-Gold option is not legal when unaffordable. Card
selection uses persistent deck-instance IDs, and priced removals
continue through the serialized event state.

**Metamorphosis**: 2-cost, exhaust-on-use Event Skill creates three
Silent Attack cards (five upgraded) with zero cost for this combat,
inserted at pseudorandom draw-pile positions. The generation pool
contains only implemented solo Silent attacks, and the emulator
uses its `combat` RNG stream. Native `CombatCardGeneration`
RNG/call order remain a known fidelity gap.

**Enlightenment**: 0-cost, exhaust-on-use Event Skill reduces other
cards' hand costs to at most 1; base effect expires on turn end or
when played, while the upgraded card's effect lasts for the combat.
No cost increase is applied to already-free cards.

## New Glory event mechanics

**Hungry for Mushrooms**: Big Mushroom grants +20 Max HP (with
the same increase in current HP), reduces first-turn hand draw by
two in every combat; Fragrant Mushroom damages owner for 15
unblockable HP and upgrades up to two distinct upgradable
persistent deck cards, randomly drawn from the event's eligible
subset. Fragrant's random subset uses the niche RNG stream.

Event-only relics are not added to regular shop/reward pools.

## Coverage boundaries

Configured solo Hive now supports six events, Glory three.
Native complete event-bag distribution/eligibility, native RNG
stream timing, other act events and Ancients remain unsupported.
No cross-character/multiplayer mechanics were introduced.
Some sources' choice resolution and relic event hooks may be
sensitive to source ordering; native capture parity remains
unavailable.

Tests target event choices, serialization-safe card selection,
eligibility and rewards, and card/relic behavior. The event
source registry keeps unsupported content explicit, never
routing to unrelated prototype events.
