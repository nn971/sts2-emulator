# v0.111.0 mechanics gap — pass 038

This pass completes the final unresolved **Overgrowth normal encounter** by implementing Fogmog,
Eye With Teeth, and a reusable summon/minion/revive lifecycle.

## Generic summoned-enemy lifecycle

Enemy moves may now summon a combat-local enemy definition. Summons receive:

- a new enemy instance ID;
- source-backed HP rolling from the same enemy definition layer;
- starting powers;
- an appended formation position;
- an optional leader identity when the summoned definition is a minion.

A newly summoned enemy skips the rest of the enemy-action pass in which it entered combat.

Minions tied to a leader are removed when the leader dies. Enemy definitions may also opt into a
revive-on-enemy-turn lifecycle: if defeated while their leader is alive, they revive to full HP at
their next enemy-action opportunity, clear ordinary status/debuff state, and skip that action.

This is generic infrastructure rather than a Fogmog-specific branch.

## Eye With Teeth

Pinned v0.111.0 behavior represented here:

- 6 HP;
- starts with Illusion and Minion markers;
- Distract adds 3 Dazed to the discard pile;
- is summoned by Fogmog rather than present in the initial formation;
- does not act on the same enemy turn in which it is summoned;
- if defeated while Fogmog lives, revives to full HP at its next action opportunity and skips that
  action;
- disappears permanently when Fogmog dies.

## Fogmog

Pinned v0.111.0 values:

- HP 74 at A0–A7, 78 at A8+;
- Illusory Spores summons Eye With Teeth;
- Thwack deals 8 damage (9 at A9+) and gains 1 Strength;
- Headbutt deals 14 damage (16 at A9+).

The source-shaped move graph is represented rather than flattened:

1. Illusory Spores;
2. forced Thwack;
3. weighted branch: Thwack weight 2, Headbutt weight 3;
4. a branch Thwack forces Headbutt next;
5. Headbutt forces Thwack next before returning to the weighted branch.

Exact native RNG state/call-order parity is not claimed.

FOGMOG_NORMAL begins with Fogmog alone; the Eye enters through the runtime summon effect.

## Regressions

Focused tests cover:

- Fogmog and Eye values;
- summon timing;
- no same-turn Distract after summon;
- 3-Dazed Distract;
- Eye death, one skipped action, and full-HP revival;
- Fogmog starting alone in the encounter;
- Fogmog Strength accumulation through Thwack.

This closes model + formation fidelity for all twelve Overgrowth normal encounters.
