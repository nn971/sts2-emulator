Horn reuses shared RelicAcquired card grants. Flesh extends the existing energy
reset modifier with a minimum-turn condition; it does not add energy as a
post-reset callback. Blood uses the existing hand-draw modifier. The Hive
opening fixture returns to floor-zero map choice without recording a map room.
Tears stores its flag on the owning combat relic instance. Its capture runs at
the beginning of the existing end-hand stage, before hand effects, matching
BeforeSideTurnEnd. Its gain uses the shared PlayerSideTurnStarted dispatch.
The flag is overwritten at each side end and resets when the next combat builds
fresh combat relic state. Existing automatic pipeline cursor indices are retained.
The broader initial/end-turn hook-stage audit remains open.
Wing adds explicit sacrifice alternatives beside ordinary card-reward skips.
Only sacrifice advances the saved counter; ordinary skip and taking cards do
not. The second sacrifice uses the shared restricted front grab bag and ordinary
RelicAcquired and acquisition deck-choice hooks. Offer selection remains resolved
while any acquisition choice is pending. Full ordinary relic pool coverage and
source RNG certification remain separate fidelity work.
Claw uses the shared enchant eligibility and RelicAcquired hooks. The pinned
model's acquisition implementation uses amount 1 despite its hover-tip amount
2. Goopy's additive block applies before ordinary block multipliers, and its
growth runs at CardPlayed completion. Temporary cards without a persistent
deck identity update only their combat enchantment.
Growth uses the ordinary relic deck-choice continuation with a relic-specified
Clone enchantment. Clone has no play-time effect: each rest copies every marked
deck instance and preserves its upgrade, enchantment and serialized card state.
Copies receive new IDs, pass through ordinary card-added hooks and remain
eligible for later Clone rests.
Eye tracks manual card plays separately from autoplay, exhausts the hand at the
early side-turn-end boundary, and resumes at the next player-turn stages without
running enemy actions or committing new enemy intents. Whispering Earring's
source first-turn suppression is represented. Full multiplayer participant
ownership remains outside the single-player target.
Legion saves one of the four source skins on its relic instance and stores the
combat pet identity in the combat snapshot. Ready card block is doubled after
ordinary card and power modifiers; a positive block gain associates Legion
with the completed card play and starts its two-player-turn cooldown. Skin
selection is deterministic and isolated from shared RNG cursors, but its
relic-owned source RNG derivation still needs native certification.

## Remaining integration gates

Pael's source pools are ordered: Flesh/Horn/Tears; Wing with conditional
Claw and Tooth, then duplicate that entire list and append Growth; Eye/Blood
with Legion only if the player has no event pet. Each pool consumes one native
event-context NextItem draw. `BeginPaelOpening` builds these pools from the
deck and pet progression, samples all three offers in order, stores the event
cursor, and the state invariant recomputes both the offers and cursor. Legal
actions retain the sampled order. Configured run-start assignments dispatch
this opening during Act 1→Hive. Native UpFront assignment and cursor
certification remain open.

Pael and Overgrowth's Byrdonis Nest now share the source `Player.HasEventPet`
predicate. `AddsPet` is modeled independently from combat-pet spawning, and the
source checks every relic instance plus an unhatched Byrdonis Egg.

Twenty-eight focused tests and the 1,693-test suite passed at this document's
milestone. A persisted Pael assignment now dispatches the Hive opening through
the configured route. Native UpFront selection and trace validation remain
open.
