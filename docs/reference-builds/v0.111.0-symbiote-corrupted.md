## Implemented route

- Symbiote is available in Hive and Glory, matching its native
  `CurrentActIndex > 0` eligibility.
- **Approach** enchants one eligible Attack with Corrupted. The other
  choice transforms one removable deck card. When no Attack can take the
  enchantment, the emulator omits the native locked Approach display and
  leaves the transform choice available.
- Corrupted multiplies powered Attack damage by 1.5 and causes 2 direct HP
  loss when the card is played. The loss ignores Block and still respects
  Buffer and automatic death prevention.
- The event retains its result page after either deck choice resolves.

## Remaining differences

- Integer combat damage rounds the Corrupted multiplier down; native damage
  uses decimal values.
- Random transformation draws from the prototype reward-card pool and stream.
  Native character-pool filtering, card generation, and RNG parity remain
  unverified.
- The `Move` value property on the native self-damage command has no separate
  emulator hook. Locked-option presentation and native oracle parity are also
  unverified.
