# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 109 — Pen Nib

Pen Nib is an Uncommon shared relic. It counts Attacks played by its owner
across combats and doubles the powered damage of the tenth Attack, including
every hit in that card play. Its saved counter wraps to zero after the tenth
Attack; melted copies are inactive.

The emulator observes `CardPlayed` after resolving the card's damage, so the
damage hook checks for a saved count of nine and the completion event then
advances and wraps the persistent counter. The executable fixture checks the
tenth-Attack multiplier, the wrapped saved counter, the following Attack, and
a melted copy.
