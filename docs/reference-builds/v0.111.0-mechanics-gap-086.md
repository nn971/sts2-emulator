# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 086 — Doll Room

Doll Room is integrated as an Act 2 Hive event for the Silent route. The free
Random choice grants one of the three doll relics. Take Some Time costs 5 HP
and offers two shuffled dolls; Examine costs 15 HP and offers all three.
Daughter of the Wind grants 1 Block after each Attack, Mr. Struggles damages
all enemies by the current turn number at the start of each player turn, and
Bing Bong copies every newly added deck card to the bottom of the deck while
preserving the card instance's persistent state.

Executable coverage resolves the free, 5 HP, and 15 HP paths, verifies the
two-choice and three-choice offers and their relic pickups, and checks that
Bing Bong copies a newly granted card with a distinct instance ID. The copy
hook is wired through deck addition paths so later event, shop, and combat
reward card grants also receive a card ID for the copy. Native event RNG,
relic offer sampling, and live combat parity remain unverified.
