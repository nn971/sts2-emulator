# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 147 — Nunchaku persistent Attack count

The pinned Nunchaku saves `AttacksPlayed` on the relic. It increments for each
owner Attack and grants 1 energy each time that count reaches another multiple
of ten during combat. Its counter and threshold therefore carry across combats.

The emulator now persists the generic relic trigger count as
`AttacksPlayed`, wrapping its saved value modulo ten. It triggers on every tenth
owner Attack and grants energy after the card-play effects resolve. The
prototype stores only the remainder rather than the source's total historical
count, which is sufficient for its trigger cadence but not its exact display
counter. No new executable coverage claim is recorded.
