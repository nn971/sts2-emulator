# v0.111.0 Glory elite slice — Soul Nexus and Mecha Knight

Source pin: `Flaaax/STS2-source@706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20`;
game build `v0.111.0/41cef1ea`. The executable models below are
**forced-combat fixtures only** (`Weight=0`, Act 3). Live-game
replay parity and native Glory map/elite selection remain outstanding.

## Reviewed source and implemented semantics

- `src/Core/Models/Monsters/SoulNexus.cs` and
  `src/Core/Models/Encounters/SoulNexusElite.cs`:
  234 HP (254 at A8+). Forced opener **Soul Burn** 29/31 (A9+);
  then uniform nonrepeating choice among Soul Burn, **Maelstrom**
  6/7 damage x4, and **Drain Life** 18/19 damage followed by
  Vulnerable 2 and Weak 2. The next intent is committed into
  combat state and remains stable under observation/fork.
- `src/Core/Models/Monsters/MechaKnight.cs` and
  `src/Core/Models/Encounters/MechaKnightElite.cs`:
  300 HP (320 at A8+), initial Artifact 3.
  **Charge** 25/30 → **Flamethrower** 8/12 and 4 Burn into hand →
  **Windup** 15 Block and Strength 5 →
  **Heavy Cleave** 35/40 → repeat from Flamethrower.
  A9+ is the Deadly Enemies breakpoint for damage.
- `src/Core/Models/Cards/Burn.cs`:
  Burn is unplayable, unupgradable, and inflicts 2 unpowered damage
  if still in hand at the player's turn end, using the shared
  combat hand-effect pipeline.

The pinned `Glory.GenerateAllEncounters()` includes three elites:
Soul Nexus, Mecha Knight, and Knights. **Knights Elite remains
unimplemented** in this slice.

## Tests and limits

`V111GloryElitesTests` checks A0/A8/A9 move and HP values,
encounter gating, nonrepeating committed Soul Nexus intents,
fork determinism, Mecha Knight's fixed cycle and Artifact,
four distinct generated Burn cards, card unplayability and
end-of-turn Burn damage.

The emulator currently uses prototype combat RNG rather than
the native MonsterAi RNG owner. Source-specific
animation/callback timing, effects on unusual card modifiers,
native-generated status ordering at hand capacity, and live
decision-boundary traces still require audit. Existing
single-player, cross-character and multiplayer scope restrictions
remain unchanged. These models do **not** unlock configured Glory
routing and do **not** establish native fidelity.
