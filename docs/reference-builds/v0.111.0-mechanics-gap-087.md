# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 087 — Crystal Sphere

Crystal Sphere is integrated in the configured Act 2 Hive and Act 3 Glory
routes. Its source eligibility requires at least 100 gold. Uncover Future
spends the event's rolled 51–99 gold cost and grants three divinations;
Payment Plan adds Debt and grants six. Debt loses up to 10 gold when held at
turn end.

The minigame persists an 11×11 board, item footprints, cleared cells, the
selected reveal tool, remaining picks and item discovery order in the event
state. The large tool clears a 3×3 area per pick, the small tool clears one
cell, and the four corners begin with their surrounding fog cleared. Fully
revealed items provide one relic, two Common and one Rare potion, three card
reward groups (each with three choices at Common, Uncommon or Rare), one Doubt
Curse, five 10-gold rewards and two 30-gold rewards. Doubt enters the deck as
soon as its item is uncovered; other rewards resolve after the final pick.
Card choices pass through Silver Crucible's reward upgrade hook.

Executable coverage resolves both event choices across the Act 2 and Act 3
routes. It checks the gold payment, Debt insertion, 3/6 pick counts, immediate
Doubt insertion, gold reward choice and finished event page. Native board
placement and item reward sampling still use prototype RNG and the supported
Silent card, potion and relic pools; native stream parity is unverified.
