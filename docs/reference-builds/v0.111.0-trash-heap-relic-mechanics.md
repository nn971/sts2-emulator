# v0.111.0 — Trash Heap event-relic mechanic pass

Source: `Flaaax/STS2-source@706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20`,
`MawBank.cs`, `DreamCatcher.cs`, `TheBoot.cs` and `TrashHeap.cs`.

This pass adds executable effects to three previously metadata-only relics
obtainable through Trash Heap's source-defined five-relic Dive pool. It does
not expand the ordinary relic bag or change the chance of receiving a relic.

Under native v111 run ownership, entering Trash Heap now creates and persists
its `TRASH_HEAP` event-local stream. `Grab` and a surviving `Dive` draw their
ordered reward indices from that stream; a lethal Dive still consumes no item
draw. The six-stream prototype profile retains its existing `event` draws.
Seeded Trash Heap result and cursor parity remain unverified.

## Maw Bank

The pinned relic gives **12 gold** whenever a new **base room** is entered,
until the player makes their first **positive-gold merchant purchase**. The
used-up flag is saved persistently on the relic instance. Merely entering
or exiting a shop does not consume it; card removal *does* count as a
merchant purchase if its price is positive.

The emulator now performs room-entry relic gold effects before the newly
entered room's normal semantics (including shop repricing/selection).
The gain does not retroactively trigger for the Trash Heap event in which
Maw Bank is obtained.

## Dream Catcher

Resting to **heal** adds a separate three-option ordinary card reward after
the HP gain. Taking or skipping a card resolves the reward and completes
the existing rest site; no additional map room is visited.

Training max HP, upgrading at the rest site and other choices do not
trigger Dream Catcher. Multiple identical relic instances, if introduced
by a synthetic test, yield separate card-reward groups.

The current card population is `PickRewardCards` from the existing Silent
prototype, rather than a claim of exact native
`CardCreationOptions.ForRoom(player, RoomType.Monster)` RNG parity.

## The Boot

If a player-powered attack penetrates Block and would cause **1–4 HP
loss**, the minimum becomes **5**. This applies per hit, including replay
hits, and after Block absorption; it is not a minimum *card damage
value*. Fully blocked attacks, status damage, poison ticks and self-HP
loss do not trigger. Enemy HP-loss caps (such as Intangible) are processed
**after** the minimum is raised.

## Still unsupported

Hand Drill is the fourth Trash Heap-only relic; it needs a generic
`AfterBlockBroken` dispatch preserving breaker/target attribution.
Its 2 Vulnerable on nonplayer targets is not yet executed. In
particular, do not substitute the per-attack damage modifier
for a genuine block-break hook.

The three added mechanics have deterministic tests but have **not**
been checked against real oracle playthroughs. The previously deferred
cross-character Trash Heap card effects remain explicitly unsupported.

## S0 stochastic boundary: Trash Heap relic reward

The `event.trash_heap.relic_reward.index_draw` migration row owns the
site-specific ordered relic pool, survival gate, and occurrence. In native
v111 runs the event uses its persisted `TRASH_HEAP` context; legacy runs use
the prototype `event` stream. The eligible relic pool and the event's
survival state are private constraints. The player-visible result is the
committed relic offer, while pool order and RNG cursor stay outside the
ordinary observation. No Nature-view law query, privileged draw transcript,
or atomic ReferenceNature session covers this reward site.

The existing event action resolves Grab or a surviving Dive synchronously;
a lethal Dive returns before the item draw. The mechanics commit the resulting
offer into EventState and the ordinary event reward choice then applies it.
Probability provenance remains **UNVERIFIED**, separate from the persisted
native event cursor. Until the saved support and replayable effect boundary
are implemented, any controlled Nature request for this site must fail closed
without publishing a partial event or substituting another provider.
