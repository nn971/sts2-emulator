Pinned sources: `Flaaax/STS2-source@706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20`,

## Source-backed bounds

| Room | A0–A2 | A3+ (Poverty) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal encounter | 10–20 | 7–15 |
| Elite | 35–45 | 26–33 |
| Boss | 100 | 75 |

`EncounterModel` supplies these defaults. From Ascension 3 onward
`Poverty` applies a 0.75 multiplier **to each endpoint individually**,
then truncates to integer. `GoldReward.Populate` draws an inclusive
uniform integer from the resulting interval with the player's Rewards RNG.
Normal combat scales *both* endpoints by `CombatRoom.GoldProportion`
using float multiplication and midpoint-to-even rounding. A normal
combat with proportion zero omits the gold reward and consumes no gold
RNG draw. Elite and boss rewards use the unscaled interval.
The first Overgrowth combat profile has no implemented escaping enemies;
its combat gold proportion is therefore 1.

The restricted emulator rolls this amount on its Rewards stream
before generating card reward options, stores the offer as
`RewardState.GoldOption`, and **does not** change the player's gold
until they perform `take_reward_gold`. Gold collection is independent
of the card/potion/relic selection order, and the action disappears once
taken. An early `leave_reward` can abandon any unclaimed normal combat
rewards, including gold. These are player-visible actions and the AI
observation exposes the offered amount and whether it is resolved.
Forked states reproduce the same reward offer and independent collection.
The older prototype map-generation profile continues to use its legacy
auto-credit economics.

## Deliberate remaining fidelity gaps

Native rewards appear as independent selectable buttons rather than the
prototype's ordered card/potion/relic subchoices. Gold pickup and
early skip are now independent, but the *other* reward types still
follow the prototype selection order. Mandatory mid-reward deck choices
are not skippable. Native UI warnings for skipping early rewards are
not implemented. Encounter-specific gold overrides, escaped monsters,
stolen-gold recovery, gold-gain hooks, and exact native RNG call order
remain separate follow-up tasks.
