# v0.111.0 mechanics gap — pass 024

This pass promotes the first broadly reused player debuffs and uses them to complete the
source-backed **Mawler** monster model for the Act 1 combat-completeness milestone.

## Player timed-debuff kernel

The prototype now has typed player powers for:

- Vulnerable;
- Weak;
- Frail.

They are ordinary debuffs, so Artifact-style debuff blocking applies through the existing generic
path.

Their positive stack count is treated as remaining owner turns. The power decrements after the
player's `PlayerTurnEnded` event finishes. This ordering matters:

- a debuff already on the player during their turn loses one turn before the following enemy turn;
- a debuff applied during enemy actions keeps its full amount until the player finishes the next
  turn;
- end-of-turn subscribers observe the pre-decrement value.

The normal and suspended event-dispatch paths share this cleanup rule.

## Damage / Block modifiers

The reusable player-power model now has multiplicative fields for:

- outgoing player Attack damage;
- incoming attack damage to the player;
- Block gained from cards.

Pinned v0.111.0 values are:

- Weak: Attack damage × 3/4;
- Vulnerable: incoming Attack damage × 3/2;
- Frail: card Block × 3/4.

Card Block applies additive Block bonuses first and Frail afterward. Enemy attacks apply attacker-side
modifiers first and player Vulnerable afterward.

Enemy `DamagePlayer` effects now also carry an `IsAttack` flag, defaulting to true. This keeps
Vulnerable attached to attack semantics without forcing later non-attack HP loss through the same
multiplier.

## Mawler

Pinned structured data:

- HP: 72 at A0–A7, 76 at A8+;
- opening move: Claw;
- Claw: 4×2 damage, 5×2 at A9+;
- Rip and Tear: 14 damage, 16 at A9+;
- Roar: apply 3 Vulnerable.

The native move graph is represented directly with the state-machine kernel:

1. fixed Claw opener;
2. uniform random choice among Rip and Tear, Roar, and Claw;
3. Rip and Tear: `CannotRepeat`;
4. Roar: `UseOnlyOnce`;
5. Claw: `CannotRepeat`.

This is the first production Act 1 state-machine enemy added after the synthetic kernel fixture.

## Regressions

New focused tests cover:

- Weak reducing Strike's 6 damage to 4;
- Frail reducing Defend's 5 Block to 3;
- Vulnerable owner-turn countdown and attack amplification;
- Mawler A8/A9 values;
- Mawler branch repeat rules;
- native Claw opener;
- forced Roar followed by a legal random attack, including Vulnerable-amplified damage and
  `UseOnlyOnce` accounting.

The branch CI passes build, tests, prototype sweep, AI JSONL bridge self-check, and trace-diff
self-check.

## Deliberate boundary

This pass promotes **Mawler's monster model**, not the full `MAWLER_NORMAL` encounter pool entry.

Singleton formation and native pool selection remain separate tracked claims. They should be promoted
when encounter-construction / pool source is translated, rather than inferred merely from the
monster being implemented.
