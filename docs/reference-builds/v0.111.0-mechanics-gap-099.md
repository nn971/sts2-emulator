# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 099 — Bread

Bread is a Shop shared relic. At the start of the first turn of combat, it
removes 2 Energy. Starting on turn two, it increases Energy per turn by 1.
The first-turn loss cannot reduce Energy below zero. Melted copies do not
apply either change.

Executable coverage starts a 3-Energy first turn, checks the relic leaves 1
Energy, verifies 4 Energy on turns two and later, and checks that a melted
copy has no effect. It also checks Shop rarity and relic-pool integration.
