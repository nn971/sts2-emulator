# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 082 — Pumpkin Candle

Pumpkin Candle is an Act 2 Tezcatara reward. Acquisition lights five persisted
kindles. Each active kindle grants one additional Energy per turn, and each
combat exit consumes one kindle. Kindle at a rest site adds five kindles.

Executable coverage checks the Tezcatara reward and acquisition initialization,
Energy while charged, six idempotent combat exits through depletion, Kindle
after depletion, and the absence of Energy or charge loss on a melted copy.
Native Ancient assignment and RNG parity remain unverified.
