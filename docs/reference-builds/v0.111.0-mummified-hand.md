# v0.111.0 Shared Relic: Mummified Hand

Target: v0.111.0 / 41cef1ea, audited source
`Flaaax/STS2-source@706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20`.

Source: [`MummifiedHand.cs`](https://github.com/Flaaax/STS2-source/blob/706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20/src/Core/Models/Relics/MummifiedHand.cs).

Mummified Hand is Rare. After its owner plays a Power during combat, it picks
a card in that owner's hand and makes it free until the end of the turn or
until played. The emulator wires that trigger into the Silent shared relic
pool and Rare queue. Its current random selection uses playable fixed-cost
cards with positive energy cost; native selection also handles star costs,
cost modifiers, and fallback candidates. Exact selection RNG is unverified.
