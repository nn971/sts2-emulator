## White Beast Statue

White Beast Statue (native Rare relic) overrides
`ShouldForcePotionReward` for every combat room owned by its player,
including normal, elite and boss rooms.

The native `PotionRewardOdds.Roll` implementation calls
`Hook.ShouldForcePotionReward` **before** consuming the Rewards RNG
stream or changing the persistent pity value. When forced, it returns
true immediately: no potion-presence probability roll and **no** 10%
pity adjustment. Normal rolls still start at 40% and change pity by
-10 points on success or +10 points on failure, and elite rolls add
a 12.5-point temporary bonus.

The emulator now implements the hook as a data-driven relic flag
(`ForceCombatPotionReward`), adds White Beast Statue to the native
Rare relic bag and regular relic pool, and forwards the flag to the
persistent odds roll for native-structure Overgrowth. A forced potion
uses the ordinary rarity-weighted potion model selection on the
Rewards stream. It remains a player-optional potion reward, subject
to normal potion-slot handling and acquisition restrictions.
The existing legacy prototype profile retains its previous behavior.

The pinned source restricts White Beast Statue's eligibility with
`IsBeforeAct3TreasureChest`; Act 1 Overgrowth meets that restriction.
Other acts are outside the current native-map implementation.

## Related relics

Prayer Wheel already adds a second normal-combat card-reward group.
Question Card already adds one option to generated card rewards.
Silver Crucible already upgrades the first three card-reward groups,
including skipped offers. Their interactions with the forced potion
reward are independent, so White Beast Statue does not consume or
change their card-rarity RNG rules.

Exact floating-point PRNG alignment, potion acquisition hooks for
all relics, and source-specific reward transformations remain
separate fidelity work. The implementation never claims byte-identical
native RNG sequences.

## S0 stochastic boundary: potion rarity and model generation

The three legacy `potion.*` rows use separate operations. The integer rarity
ticket maps a 100-way support to 65 Common, 25 Uncommon and 10 Rare outcomes;
the pinned Native float route is a different primitive. Rarity-conditioned
model selection uses the source-ordered pool for that rarity after exclusions
are applied. Uniform model selection instead indexes the full ordered
character/shared potion pool without a rarity roll. These candidate pools and
selected rarity are private generation constraints.

The player-facing projection shows the selected potion or reward, not the
model pool order, rarity ticket or RNG state. The existing controlled
event-choice continuation captures the legacy rarity and model requests for
Whispering Hollow's `gold` choice and the Uncommon model request for Potion
Courier's `ransack` choice. The run-action, combat-reward, and merchant
continuations also capture their explicitly listed legacy generation
prefixes; callers outside those boundaries remain source-owned and fail
closed for controlled requests. These continuations do not establish Native
float-route parity or complete trigger coverage. The separate
`combat.potion_generation.random_model` site has an opt-in saved semantic
operation that exposes the ordered pool to Nature and resumes each selected
potion once. Its Chance Law now records exact Native `NextInt` masses on the
declared support; pool completeness, Native stream attribution, and cursor
parity remain open. The legacy rarity/model reference laws remain
**UNVERIFIED**, distinct from cursor parity.
