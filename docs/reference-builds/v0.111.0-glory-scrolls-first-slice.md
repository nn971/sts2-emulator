## Mechanics implemented

Scrolls have HP 30–37 (A8 33–39), Chomp 14/16, Chew 5/6
twice, and More Teeth giving +2 Strength. Their state machine is
Chomp → More Teeth → Chew → weighted random Chomp/Chew
(Chomp cannot repeat), with Chew returning to the random branch.

The weak encounter includes three scrolls with a single initial
`NextInt(3)` assigning Chomp/Chew/More Teeth to each scroll
cyclically. The normal encounter adds a fourth scroll with a
fixed More Teeth opener. Exactly one encounter-local cyclic roll
occurs regardless of the number of monsters.

Paper Cuts begins at two stacks on each scroll. Every powered hit
that actually deals unblocked damage also reduces the player's
persistent maximum HP by two; blocked hits do not. The native
`LoseMaxHp` operation reduces current HP if necessary to fit
the new maximum and clamps maximum HP to at least one.

## Fidelity and scope

These registrations have `Weight=0` and remain forced-test-only.
The source audit and synthetic regressions establish an executable
slice, not real-game parity. Native Glory map/encounter bags,
context RNG and rare damage-hook interactions remain unverified.
The existing six prototype RNG streams continue to operate.
Cross-character and multiplayer work is postponed.
