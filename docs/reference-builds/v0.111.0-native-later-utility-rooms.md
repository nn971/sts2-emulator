# v0.111.0 — Hive/Glory shared utility-room routing

**Pinned:** `Flaaax/STS2-source@706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20`

Native `src/Core/Rooms/MerchantRoom.cs`,
`Entities/Merchant/MerchantInventory.cs`,
`Rooms/RestSiteRoom.cs`, `Entities/RestSite/RestSiteOption.cs`
and `Rooms/TreasureRoom.cs` route these room types without
specializing by Act 1/2/3. This patch enables these *shared*
emulator systems on opt-in full-sized native Hive and Glory maps.
It does not claim source RNG or treasure-interface parity.

## Merchant

* Five character slots: two Attacks, two Skills, one Power, with
  one on sale, followed by two colorless rarity-specific slots.
  Three distinct potions and three relics are drawn from the
  existing native-shaped restricted pools. Two relic rarities are
  rolled and the third is Shop rarity.
* Reuse `PrototypeNativeCardRarityOdds.GenerateMerchantCards`,
  `PrototypeColorlessCards.PickMerchantCard`,
  `PrototypeNativePotionShop`, `PrototypeNativeRelicGrabBag`
  and native card/potion/relic price rules; no generic
  six-floor shop inventory silently leaks through.
* Run-wide card removal count and Ascension 6 inflation are
  conserved across Hive and Glory. Shop entry relic hooks,
  purchase hooks, potion slot replacement and persistent
  relic bag state continue to run.
* Shop `leave_shop` records one completed room and returns to the
  native-map successor options.

## Rest site

* Native `RestSiteOption.Generate` always provides Heal and
  Smith for the single-player base options; eligible relics
  provide their own extra options. Shared Hatch remains available
  when the player's active relic and pet-slot rules permit.
* Rest heals 30% max HP (the existing integer approximation),
  invokes the rest-site heal hook and its existing reward
  continuation. Smith upgrades a chosen eligible persistent
  deck card by **one level**, not hard-setting it to level 1.
* **The legacy prototype's `rest_train` +4 MaxHP option is
  intentionally absent** on source-profiled maps. Requesting
  it directly fails rather than creating a non-native advantage.
  The legacy six-floor prototype **and existing Act 1 native-profile\n  regression fixtures** retain their previously modeled Train behavior\n  for backwards compatibility. Their native-fidelity gap remains open.

## Treasure

* Treasures use the persistent rarity-ordered relic grab bag
  (not the generic relic ID pool) and the existing relic reward
  choice/claim flow. Silver Crucible still suppresses its
  first native treasure and records its persistent use.
* There is no implicit auto-acquisition: an opened chest with
  a relic offer enters the Reward phase; selecting it and
  leaving returns to native-map choice and completes the room.
* Full pinned chest opening animations, room-specific gold and
  extra relic-added rewards, and native RPG networking
  interfaces are outside this patch.

## Unknown rooms

The shared room-type resolver can now produce Combat, Event,
Shop or Treasure in later acts. The current prototype odds/pity
and discrete `event`-stream sampling are **not** validated
against the native Unknown room algorithm.

## Known gates

Production v111 Act 1→Hive transitions, native act-entry Ancients,
all-unlocked card and relic availability, native RNG/call-order,
character-specific rest options, and actual real-game oracle
replay remain incomplete. The registered/implemented card,
potion, relic and rest-hook subsets have explicit limitations;
only the supported mechanics are exercised here.

## Regression coverage

The patch tests each shared utility room in Hive and Glory:
full seven-card/three-potion/three-relic inventory, sale and
purchase, removal price at Ascension 6, deterministic forks,
rest Heal and Smith and exclusion of Train, fixed treasury
row's persistent draw/claim, and deterministic forcing of
unknown nodes to Shop or Treasure. All tests validate their
resulting run state and preserve the original input snapshot.
