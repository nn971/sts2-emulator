## S0 stochastic boundary: `event.unknown_room.odds.ticket`

The row belongs to entering an Unknown map node. Its private constraints are
the current act's evolving Monster, Treasure and Shop weights, the selected
node's act/floor identity, and whether a room rule forces the result to Event.
The emulator uses a bounded 1000-way integer ticket on the legacy `event`
stream, initially mapping weights 100/20/30 to Monster/Treasure/Shop and the
remainder to Event.
After a non-forced result, the matching Monster, Treasure or Shop weight
resets to its base; each nonmatching weight increases by its base. An Event
result increases all three weights. A forced-Event node still consumes the
ticket but skips the odds update.

The pinned native `UnknownMapPointOdds.Roll` uses a unit-float draw from the
source's `UnknownMapPoint` stream and applies its own allowed-room filtering
and odds state. The emulator ticket is a bounded-integer approximation; it
does not claim source stream, tutorial-override, blacklist, float-edge, or
cursor parity.

The player-visible projection shows the Unknown marker before entry and the
resolved room after entry, not the current odds, ticket, or prior hidden
rolls. Room selection is resolved synchronously while opening the node.
This operation has no persisted ReferenceNature continuation, Nature-view
Chance Law, or privileged per-roll transcript; controlled requests must fail
closed.
Its reference law remains **UNVERIFIED**.
