# v0.111.0 native damage-only HP thresholds: Ceremonial Beast and Terror Eel

Pinned source: `Flaaax/STS2-source@706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20`.

## Source behavior

Both of these powers override
`PowerModel.AfterDamageReceived(PlayerChoiceContext, Creature,
DamageResult, ValueProp, ...)`. Their phase interrupt depends on the
specific *damage callback* and cannot be reduced to an
`HP <= threshold` predicate over every kind of HP change.

| Encounter | Source power | Exact trigger | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CeremonialBeastBoss | `PlowPower` | `result.UnblockedDamage > 0 && target.CurrentHp <= base.Amount` | Remove TemporaryStrength/Strength and Plow; `CeremonialBeast.SetStunned()` and `CreatureCmd.Stun(..., StunnedMove, BeastCryState.StateId)` |
| TerrorEelElite | `ShriekPower` | `result.UnblockedDamage > 0 && target.CurrentHp <= base.Amount` | `CreatureCmd.Stun(..., TerrorState.StateId)` and remove Shriek |

The pin gives 150/160 Plow at A0/A9+, and 70/75 Shriek
at A0/A8+. The source's interrupt does **not** require the HP
threshold to have been crossed by the particular hit: if poison
has already put HP below that threshold, the next unblocked
damage still triggers it.

A fully blocked hit has zero unblocked damage and does not
trigger either power. Direct LoseHp, poison and other raw HP loss
do not invoke `AfterDamageReceived`. Importantly, the source
retains each power during those raw HP transitions even after
HP drops below its numeric threshold.

## Emulator correction

Previously `ModifyEnemyHpLoss` applied these threshold
effects to **every** HP-loss operation, including poison and
direct LoseEnemyHp. This could stun bosses too soon and silently
alter the announced move and phase progression.

The strict experimental native
`CeremonialBeastBoss`/`TerrorEelElite` path now calls
`ModifyEnemyHpLoss(..., allowDamageTriggeredThreshold: false)`
for raw HP loss. Normal unblocked damage, through
`DamageEnemyInternal`, still evaluates the existing
threshold interrupt, including the one-shot power removal,
Stun intent and (for Plow) Strength clearing.

No new RNG draws or creature-context replacements occur. The
prototype default, including its older broad HP-loss trigger,
is unchanged for compatibility with existing AI training.

## Verification

`NativeDamageOnlyPhaseTriggersV111Tests` checks eight independent
entry seeds for each of A0/A8/A10 for each encounter (48 cases):

1. Actual native opt-in elite/boss room entry and valid sidecar.
2. Raw HP loss crossing the threshold leaves original phase/power.
3. Fully blocked hit leaves phase/power unchanged.
4. First unblocked hit *after the HP is already below threshold*
   removes exactly the audited threshold power and precommits Stun.
5. Plow additionally removes Strength; Shriek does not.
6. Both run-level niche and monster AI cursors remain unchanged,
   snapshots and original inputs are immutable.
7. The default prototype's previous raw-HP behavior is retained.

## Scope

Only these **HP-threshold interrupt triggers** are source-audited.
Complete native damage pipelines, power callbacks, visual stun effects,
boss move resolution, shop/reward/encounter selection and live-game
trace parity are not implied. No cross-character/multiplayer features
are changed.
