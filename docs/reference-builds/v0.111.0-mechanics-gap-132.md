# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 132 — Punch Dagger and Momentum

Punch Dagger is a Shop relic. On pickup, its owner enchants one Attack in the
deck with Momentum at amount 5. Momentum adds its accumulated bonus to powered
Attack damage and gains another 5 after each play. The first hit has no Momentum
bonus; later hits from the same combat card gain 5, 10, and so on. The running
bonus belongs to the combat card instance and is not stored on the persistent
deck card.

Executable coverage checks that Punch Dagger offers only Attack cards, applies
Momentum +5, and that two executions deal 6 then 11 damage while leaving the
persistent enchantment amount at 5.
