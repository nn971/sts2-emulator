# v0.111.0 mechanics gap — pass 019

This pass adds a reusable constrained-random enemy move policy and uses Flail Knight as the
source-backed acceptance case.

## Generic move policy

Enemy definitions can now choose between:

- the existing sequential opener/loop policy;
- `UniformRandomAfterOpener`.

The random policy has:

- an explicit opening move index;
- persistent last-move identity;
- persistent consecutive-use count;
- per-move maximum consecutive uses;
- uniform choice among the moves still legal after repeat filtering.

The prototype combat RNG stream supplies the random choice, so runs remain deterministic for a
fixed emulator seed.

## Flail Knight

The production A0 definition now has:

- 101 HP;
- opener Ram for 15 damage;
- War Chant: gain 3 Strength, max one consecutive use;
- Flail: 9 damage twice, max two consecutive uses;
- Ram: 15 damage, max two consecutive uses.

After the opener, War Chant, Flail, and Ram are uniformly selected from the currently legal set.

## Enemy self powers

Enemy effects can now apply a power to the acting enemy.

The first source-backed use is War Chant applying `proto.power.strength`.

Power definitions can contribute a flat bonus to enemy attack damage per stack. Strength contributes
one damage per stack to every hit, so Flail receives the bonus independently on both hits.

This is generic enemy-power damage modification rather than a Flail-specific branch.

## Tests

Coverage pins:

- the static Flail Knight definition;
- the opener and repeat constraints;
- a deterministic seeded random sequence;
- long-run enforcement of the one/two-use repeat limits;
- persistent Strength accumulation;
- per-hit Strength damage on later Flail/Ram attacks.

## Remaining boundary

The A0 random policy is modeled. Native RNG-stream parity is still a separate oracle/differential
testing problem, and Ascension 9 damage values remain outside the current run model.
