# Fake Merchant event

Source pin: `Flaaax/STS2-source@706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20`.
Relevant source models are `Events/FakeMerchant.cs`,
`Encounters/FakeMerchantEventEncounter.cs`, `Monsters/FakeMerchantMonster.cs`,
`Potions/FoulPotion.cs`, and the ten `Fake*` relic classes.

The configured Silent route now includes the shared Fake Merchant in Hive and
Glory. It follows the source eligibility gate (second Act or later, and either
100 gold or a Foul Potion), rolls six of the nine 50-gold fake relics, removes
an item from stock after purchase, and keeps the leave option available.
Throwing a Foul Potion consumes it and starts the event-only single Fake
Merchant fight. The merchant has 165 HP, gains 10 HP at Tough Enemies
ascension, and uses Swipe, Spew Coins (eight hits of 2), Throw Relic (9 damage
and 1 Frail), and Enrage (2 Strength). At Deadly Enemies ascension Swipe and
Throw Relic gain 2 and 1 damage respectively. Victory offers 300 gold, Fake
Merchant's Rug, and each relic still in stock; the event does not resume after
the fight.

The nine purchasable relics implement their source effects: 4 opening Block,
1 HP on the first player turn, 1 energy every five player-side turns, 10% Max
HP healing on pickup, 3 Max HP, 3 Block when ending the turn with no Block,
randomized drawn-card costs from 0 to 3, +1 damage on Strike-tagged Attacks,
and +1 energy in the first combat after a Rest Site. The Rug has no additional
effect. Foul Potion deals 12 damage to all enemies when used in combat and has
no self-damage.

The event uses prototype event/combat RNG and the shared event-action/reward
model; native stream parity, save compatibility with the game, and native
replay validation remain open. Multiplayer behavior is outside the configured
single-player route.
