# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 050 — Centennial Puzzle

Pinned `CentennialPuzzle.cs` is a Common relic. Its first
`AfterDamageReceived` callback with positive `UnblockedDamage` during combat
marks the relic used and draws three cards. `AfterCombatEnd` resets the
per-combat flag.

The emulator adds a `PlayerDamaged` combat event and wires it to enemy attack
damage, end-of-turn damage, combat damage effects, damage retaliation, and
Galvanic damage. Centennial Puzzle listens to the event and draws three cards;
its relic trigger counter limits it to once per combat. The relic is included
in the common rarity bag and ordinary shared relic pool.

Damage raised by the enemy-action resolver is currently dispatched after that
enemy-action batch completes. Exact mid-batch callback order remains for the
later fidelity pass.
