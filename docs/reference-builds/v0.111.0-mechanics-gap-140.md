The merchant restocks the same entry after a successful purchase. Character
card entries retain their card type, colorless entries retain rarity, potion
entries generate one potion, and relic entries roll a new rarity while
excluding relics already displayed. The card-removal entry's restock method
does not restore its one-use removal.

The emulator now places The Courier in the shared Rare relic queue and keeps
it out of merchant relic offers. Its typed 4/5 price multiplier applies to
cards, potions, relics, and card removal. Purchases refill the same offer ID:
card slots retain type (and colorless slots retain rarity), potion slots
generate a new potion, and relic slots draw an unowned shop-eligible relic.
Refilled entries are repriced with all active relic modifiers. Potion offers
may duplicate while The Courier is held, matching the source's fresh
single-potion refill population.

Executable coverage reprices a shop, buys one card, potion, and relic, and
checks that each slot refills at its discounted price. It also verifies Rare
pool membership and shop exclusion. Exact native shop RNG order and price
jitter are not claimed here. Reproduce with
`python tools/check_v111_coverage.py` after building the Release CLI.
