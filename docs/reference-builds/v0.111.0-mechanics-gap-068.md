# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 068 — History Course

Pinned `HistoryCourse.cs` is an Event-rarity Glory relic obtained by freeing
Repy from the cage in War Historian Repy. Starting on turn 2, it auto-plays a
free duplicate of the last completed Attack from the previous player turn,
excluding an attack that was itself a duplicate. The emulator implements the
replay through its opening/turn auto-preplay stage. Melted copies are now
excluded from both attack-history capture and replay activation.

Coverage checks the event-only cage reward and executes a turn-two Silent
Neutralize replay, verifying the duplicate, Weak, damage, and unchanged Energy.
The melted-relic case remains inactive.
