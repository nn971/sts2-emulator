# Pinned v0.111.0 normal-encounter roster closure

Game build v0.111.0 / 41cef1ea; audited code
`Flaaax/STS2-source@706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20`.
Inspect the matching files under
`src/Core/Models/{Acts,Encounters,Monsters,Powers}/`.

The native eligible identifier inventory contains **41** encounters
whose names end in `Normal`. Before this patch 34 were registered.
The final seven are:

- `AxebotsNormal`: a single two-Stock Axebot, with its two
  additional bodies, changed HP and postrespawn BootUp.
- `ConstructMenagerieNormal`: fixed Punch Construct + two
  Cubex Constructs. Reuses the registered Act 1 monster models.
- `FabricatorNormal`: fixed central Fabricator. At fewer than
  four live allies, randomly either summons defense and aggression
  bots or attacks and summons an aggressive bot. At four allies it
  attacks without summoning. Defense pool = Guardbot, Noisebot;
  offense pool = Zapbot, Stabbot. A given bot model is excluded
  from the immediate next summon roll. Four named bot slots;
  minions do not prolong combat after their parent dies.
  Guardbot grants 15 Block to Fabricator; Noisebot generates
  Dazed in discard and random draw; Zapbot starts with
  High Voltage 2 and gains Strength on enemy-side ends;
  Stabbot attacks and applies Frail.
- `GlobeHeadNormal`: Galvanic-6/8 marks Power cards and inflicts
  unpowered damage on play. Shocking Slap, Thunder Strike and
  Galvanic Burst follow the source's three-move cycle.
- `OwlMagistrateNormal`: Scrutiny, six Peck hits, Judicial
  Flight (+Soar), and Verdict (+Vulnerable, -Soar). Soar halves
  powered attack damage received while active.
- `TheLostAndForgottenNormal`: two monsters with independent
  Strength/Dexterity theft, growth, and owner-death restitution.
- `TunnelerNormal`: Chomper opening with Scream and a Tunneler.
  Its source class is real but `Hive.GenerateAllEncounters`
  does not contain it. This is deliberately only a forced fixture.

Each new encounter has `Weight=0`; no native Act 2/3 map or
encounter-bag integration is claimed. Existing Act 1 normal
registrations remain integrated. Coverage statuses are intentionally
`partial` and validation remains unverified; complex timing,
animations, full native RNG ownership and live-game reference
traces remain outstanding.
