# Glory elite/boss roster completion slice (v0.111.0)

Pinned game v0.111.0, build 41cef1ea; source
`Flaaax/STS2-source@706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20`.

This source-backed combat slice registers **all three native Glory elites**
(Knights, Mecha Knight, Soul Nexus) and **all three Glory bosses**
(Queen, Test Subject, Aeonglass). The last two elites were implemented
in the stacked predecessor. All six are **Weight=0, forced-test-only**
until native Glory map, encounter pool, and RNG ownership are integrated.

## New encounters

- **Knights Elite**: Flail Knight (Ram opener, weighted War Chant/Flail/Ram),
  Spectral Knight (Hex → Slash then weighted Slash/Flame),
  and Magi Knight (Power Shield → Dampen → Ram → Prep → Bomb → Ram).
  Three independently targetable named slots, native A8 HP and A9 moves.
  Hex source-bound afflicts cards with Ethereal, Dampen temporarily
  suppresses upgrades and restores them after its last source dies.
- **Queen Boss**: Queen 400/419 HP and Torch Head Amalgam 199/211 HP
  occupy separate slots. Amalgam uses source-defined five-move attacks.
  Queen opens Puppet Strings (Chains of Binding 3) and You Are Mine
  (99 Frail/Weak/Vulnerable), then buffs Amalgam by 1 Strength and gains
  20 Block per turn while it lives. When Amalgam dies, Queen transitions
  to five-hit Off With Your Head → Execution → Enrage +2, looping.
  Killing Amalgam while Queen has committed Burn Bright interrupts that
  intent immediately with Enrage. Chains afflicts up to three newly
  drawn cards per player turn with Bound; playing one Bound card prevents
  another Bound card play that turn and the affliction clears at turn end.
- **Test Subject Boss**: fixed initial form 100/111 HP, Bite/Skull Bash
  opener loop and Enrage +2/+3 Strength for Skill plays. Two lethal
  transitions pause ordinary damage, execute a respawn, and restore
  200/212 then 300/313 HP on the *same combat instance*. The middle
  form gains Painful Stabs, scaling its multi-claw hit count and adding
  Wounds per unblocked hit. The third form gets alternating Nemesis
  damage cap, Lacerate ×3, Big Pounce and Burning Growl (3/5 Burn,
  +2/+3 Strength). The final lethal hit is terminal.
- **Aeonglass Boss**: 512/535 HP, Artifact 3, a six-cards-played
  Withering Presence counter; Ebb → Eye Lasers → Increasing Intensity
  cycles. Intensity increments every existing Wither fake-upgrade,
  generates 1/2 more Withers and gives Strength 3/4 with +1 per
  earlier Intensity. Each held Wither deals 3 plus 3 per fake-upgrade
  in unpowered damage at player turn end.

## Coverage and source restrictions

Source-backed synthetic regressions cover A0/A8/A9, formations, initial
and conditional intents, limb/partner death, two resurrection transitions,
per-instance counters, six-card Wither creation, combat card status effects,
and fork/replay identity. The pinned inventory and executable coverage
baseline are intentionally conservative: later-act entries are
**registered/partial/unintegrated/unverified**, not native-parity validated.

Important remaining verification includes native RNG stream/call-order,
queen's Bound acquisition timing and card-duplication edge cases,
Magi Knight's Dampen restoration under multiple casters, Test Subject
special death animations and Intangible timing, Aeonglass special
interactions with transformed/copied Withers, and high-capacity hand
insertion ordering. This slice excludes multiplayer and cross-character
content, preserves no prototype replacements in real v111 routes, and
makes no claim of complete Act 3 fidelity.
