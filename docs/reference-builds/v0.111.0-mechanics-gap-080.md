# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 080 — Toasty Mittens

Toasty Mittens is an Act 2 Tezcatara reward. On the opening player turn and
each later player turn, its owner exhausts one chosen card from hand and gains
one Strength.

Executable coverage checks the Tezcatara reward and ordered trigger effects,
dispatches the opening hook and turns two and three, resolves each hand choice,
and verifies the selected cards are exhausted as Strength rises to three. A
melted copy does not prompt or grant Strength. Native Ancient assignment and
RNG parity remain unverified.
