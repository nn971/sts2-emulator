## Turret Operator Weak (final missing ordinary weak encounter)

- Fixed formation: Living Shield first, Turret Operator second.
- Shield has 55 HP (A8+ 65). Its opening and continuing accompanied
  action is Shield Slam, 6 damage. With no living teammate it takes
  the irreversible Smash branch: 16 damage (A9+ 18) followed by
  Strength +3, repeating Smash.
- Shield starts with Rampart 25, applied in the native source's
  `AfterAddedToRoom`. Rampart's `AfterSideTurnStart` grants 25
  unpowered Block to *each living Turret Operator* at every player
  side-turn start, including turn 1. Owner death suppresses future
  grants; separately owned Ramparts stack. Existing enemy-side Block
  clearing handles the end of a protection window.
- Turret has 41 HP (A8+ 51). It opens with 3 (A9+ 4) damage
  five times, repeats that attack, then gains +1 Strength and
  returns to the first attack.
- No new random enemy AI decisions are introduced. Move commitment
  and owner/target identity are fork-/snapshot-owned.

Synthetic regression tests cover enemy HP, formation order, first
player-side protection, repeated grants, dead-owner suppression,
ally-loss behavior, and deterministic forks. Unresolved release gates:
