# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 100 — White Star

group containing three Rare cards. It does not affect normal combats or boss
rooms. The relic leaves the Glory relic pool at the Act 3 treasure chest.

Rare-pool reward for rerolls. Coverage verifies that all three options are
Rare, the group appears only for Elite rooms and active copies, and the relic
