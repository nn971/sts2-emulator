# v0.111.0 mechanics gap — pass 012

This pass adds generic tagged-card keyword grants and per-turn tagged-card play counters, with
Phantom Blades as the pinned Silent acceptance case.

## Native evidence

Pinned v0.111.0 data gives Phantom Blades as:

- Silent Uncommon Power;
- cost 1;
- Shivs gain Retain;
- the first Shiv played each turn deals 9 additional damage;
- upgrade keeps cost 1 and raises the additional damage to 12.

The important structural points are that Retain applies to Shiv cards as a category while the power
is active, and that the damage bonus depends on how many Shivs have already completed a play this
turn.

## Tagged keyword grants

`PrototypePowerDefinition` can now grant a card keyword to cards carrying a required tag.

Phantom Blades declares:

- granted keyword: Retain;
- required card tag: `Shiv`.

`CardHasKeyword` combines:

1. the card definition's intrinsic keyword;
2. active power grants whose required tag matches;
3. combat-card instance overrides.

Instance overrides remain the final layer, so typed card-local state can still explicitly enable or
disable a keyword when future enchantment/affliction mechanics require that behavior.

This means generated Shivs need no Phantom-Blades-specific mutation when they enter Hand. End-turn
cleanup asks the ordinary effective-keyword query and retains them while the power is active.

## Per-turn tagged-card play counters

`PrototypeCombatCounters` now stores the tags of cards whose `CardPlayed` lifecycle completed
during the current turn.

The representation is generic rather than a Shiv-specific boolean. A power can therefore ask how
many completed plays with a given tag have occurred this turn.

The tag list:

- is updated by the ordinary `CardPlayed` counter event;
- is deep-copied with combat state;
- resets at the existing `AdvanceTurn` counter-reset stage.

Because the counter increments on `CardPlayed`, after the card execution rather than before its
damage effects, the first tagged card naturally sees count zero during its damage resolution.

Replay also behaves correctly without another branch:

1. first Shiv execution sees zero prior Shiv plays and receives the bonus;
2. its `CardPlayed` event records one Shiv;
3. a Replay execution sees one prior Shiv and receives no first-play bonus.

## First-tagged-play attack modifier

The player Attack modifier layer now supports an additive bonus requiring:

- a source card tag;
- zero previously completed plays with that tag this turn.

Phantom Blades stores its damage amount as power stacks: base card applies 9 stacks, upgraded card
applies 12. Its first-Shiv modifier contributes one damage per stack.

This composes with the existing attack modifier stages such as Accuracy's flat Shiv bonus and
Tracking's target-status-conditioned percentage modifier.

## Tests

Focused coverage verifies:

- Phantom Blades static card/power metadata;
- base 9 and upgraded 12 stack application;
- the first Shiv in a turn receiving the bonus;
- a second Shiv in the same turn receiving only normal damage;
- tagged play counts increasing through CardPlayed;
- tagged play counts resetting on turn advance;
- an unplayed Shiv being retained by the active power;
- the next turn's first Shiv receiving the bonus again.

The full CI suite is green.

## Remaining frontier

The continuation system still intentionally guards events emitted inside iterative draw,
damage/discard, and exhaust primitives when a subscriber would request player input. Those loops
should gain explicit frames only when pinned mechanics need them.

The next high-value static-first area is richer card-local modifier state:

- enchantments;
- afflictions;
- clone/snapshot transformations such as Nightmare's Affliction clearing;
- modifier-provided Replay/keyword/cost behavior.

The existing combat-card snapshot, Replay count, keyword overrides, and tag-query layer provide the
initial substrate for that work.
