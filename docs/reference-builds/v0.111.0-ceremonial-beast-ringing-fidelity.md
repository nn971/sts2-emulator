## Discrepancy resolved

Previously the emulator modeled Ringing as a global
`MaxCardsPlayablePerTurn: 1` modifier. It did not actually
afflict combat cards, so public observations were missing
an important decision-relevant property: if another affliction
is already present, native Ringing leaves that card unaffected,
and it may remain playable after the first played card.

Now Ringing's definition has the generic applied-card-affliction
hooks enabled with the native existing-affliction exclusion and
cleanup semantics. Those hooks handle cards entering combat
after the power was applied, including temporary Shiv cards.
The legal-action and `PlayCard` checks agree and enforce the
one-card restriction *only* on Ringing-afflicted cards; independent
card-play caps from other relics and powers are unaffected.
The normal player-turn-end power removal clears Ringing while
preserving other afflictions.

## Regression and remaining fidelity limits

`PrototypeCeremonialBeastTests` checks Beat Cry afflicts
cards; new Shivs are immediately afflicted and unplayable
after the first card play; cards with pre-existing Smog remain
playable; Ringing cleans up at turn end without deleting Smog.
It retains boss stun/Plow and move-order tests.

The reference capture was **not** a literal simulator replay:
the exact native RNG codec, action-to-action transition
alignment, and complete noncombat context are still open.
No claim of complete parity is made. The next high-value
step is event/action reconstruction for the elite encounters,
followed by concrete HP/block/power-state differential checks.
