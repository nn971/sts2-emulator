# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 121 — Dragon Fruit

Dragon Fruit is a Shop relic. After its owner gains positive gold, it grants 1
Max HP and raises current HP by the same amount. Zero or blocked gold gains do
not trigger the effect, and melted copies are inactive. Gold gained by another
player does not trigger the owner's relic.

The executable coverage fixture checks the Shop pool and rarity, a positive
gold gain, a zero gain, Ectoplasm's gold-gain block, and a melted copy. The
source also stops offering Dragon Fruit at or after the Act 3 treasure chest;
that late-run relic-eligibility filter remains open in the prototype pool.
