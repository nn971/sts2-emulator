# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 055 — Darkstone Periapt

Pinned `DarkstonePeriapt.cs` is an Event relic offered by the Act 1
Underdocks Trash Heap. When one of its owner's Curse cards enters the deck,
it grants 6 Max HP and heals 6.

The emulator's `CardAdded` run trigger requires a Curse and applies `GainMaxHp`
for 6. The coverage integration check verifies the event-only Trash Heap offer
and keeps this relic out of ordinary drops.
