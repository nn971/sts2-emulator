# v0.111.0 mechanics gap — pass 037

This pass promotes **Vine Shambler** and **Slithering Strangler**, adds reusable Tangled/Constrict mechanics, and introduces alternate encounter formation variants.

## Vine Shambler

Pinned v0.111.0 values:

- HP 61 at A0–A7, 64 at A8+;
- Swipe: 6 damage x2, 7 x2 at A9+;
- Grasping Vines: 8 damage, 9 at A9+, then applies 1 Tangled;
- Chomp: 16 damage, 18 at A9+;
- fixed cycle: Swipe -> Grasping Vines -> Chomp -> repeat.

### Tangled

Tangled is represented as a timed player debuff that afflicts every Attack with Entangled, catches new Attacks entering combat, adds +1 energy cost per Tangled stack, expires at the end of the next player turn, and removes the Entangled afflictions it supplied.

The existing generic card-affliction cost path already supported the surcharge. This pass fixes a generic lifecycle gap: player powers removed by countdown now clear their applied card afflictions using the same cleanup rule as explicit power removal.

VINE_SHAMBLER_NORMAL is represented as its native singleton formation.

## Slithering Strangler

Pinned v0.111.0 values:

- HP 53–55 at A0–A7, 54–56 at A8+;
- Constrict: apply 3 Constrict;
- Thwack: 7 damage + 5 Block, 8 damage at A9+;
- Lash: 12 damage, 13 at A9+.

The source-shaped move graph is Constrict -> equal random Thwack/Lash -> Constrict -> equal random Thwack/Lash -> repeat. The attack choice may repeat across Constrict turns.

### Constrict

Constrict is represented as a cumulative source-bound player debuff. At PlayerTurnEnded, it deals damage through Block equal to its current stack count. Reapplying Constrict adds 3 more stacks. Because the power is source-bound, defeating the Strangler removes the power before later turn-end ticks.

## Alternate formation variants

PrototypeEncounterDefinition now supports alternate formation variants. A variant can contain fixed enemy specs plus its own random selection groups. The selected variant is resolved before its internal random groups. This remains prototype RNG selection and does not claim native RNG call-order parity.

SLITHERING_STRANGLER_NORMAL uses this abstraction for three source-backed companion shapes:

1. Strangler + Snapping Jaxfruit;
2. Strangler + one random medium slime;
3. Strangler + Leaf Slime (S) + Twig Slime (S).

The positions are canonical emulator formation positions; the present claim is composition and multiplicity fidelity rather than native serialized slot identity.

## Regressions

Focused tests verify Vine Shambler scaling/cycle, Tangled Attack-only surcharge and expiry cleanup, Strangler scaling/move graph, cumulative Constrict damage and death cleanup, and reachability of all four concrete friend compositions.

## Overgrowth normal progress

Eleven of twelve normal encounters now have source-backed monster models and composition/formation representations.

The sole remaining normal encounter is FOGMOG_NORMAL.

Fogmog is the next dependency because it requires the first true in-combat enemy summon plus the Eye With Teeth Illusion revive/minion lifecycle. Once Fogmog is complete, the twelve-entry native Overgrowth normal encounter queue can be activated as an explicit no-replacement pool.
