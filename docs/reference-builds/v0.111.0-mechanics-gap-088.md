# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 088 — Welcome to Wongos

Welcome to Wongos is integrated as an Act 1 event in both Overgrowth and
Underdocks. It requires at least 100 gold. The player can spend 100 gold on a
Common shop relic, 200 gold on the Rare relic featured when the event opens,
or 300 gold on a Wongos Mystery Ticket. Leaving downgrades an upgraded deck
card when one is available. Purchases add 32, 16, or 8 points respectively to
the saved Wongos total. Crossing a 2,000-point threshold grants the Wongo
Customer Appreciation Badge, and the event preserves the matching result
page.

The Mystery Ticket counts completed combat rooms. On the fifth combat it
adds three separate relic reward groups and records that it has paid out, so
later combats cannot trigger the reward again. The Appreciation Badge has no
additional gameplay trigger in the pinned source.
