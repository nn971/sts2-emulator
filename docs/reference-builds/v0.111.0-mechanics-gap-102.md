# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 102 — Beating Remnant

Beating Remnant is a Rare shared relic. During combat, the owner can lose at
most 20 HP per turn. The counter spans the owner's turn and the following
enemy turn; it resets when the next owner turn begins. HP-loss reductions such
as Tungsten Rod are applied before this cap, and melted copies do not count.

path, checks the combined Tungsten Rod and Beating Remnant result, verifies
the per-turn reset, and confirms melted copies do not cap loss. This is
engine-path coverage, not live-game parity.
