# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 097 — Amethyst Aubergine

Amethyst Aubergine is a Common shared relic. After combat-room victories it
adds a separate 15 Gold reward, including elite and act-boss rewards, except
for the final Glory boss. Melted copies do not grant the reward. The relic is
not sold by merchants and leaves the Glory relic pool at the Act 3 treasure
chest.

The native reward flow exposes the bonus as its own optional Gold group. The
legacy combat flow credits it with the existing automatic Gold reward. Both
flows suppress the bonus when gold gain is prevented. Coverage exercises
combat, elite, act-boss, intermediate Glory boss, final Glory boss, melted,
Ectoplasm, merchant, and Act 3 treasure-pool cases.
