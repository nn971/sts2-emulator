# v0.111.0 Shared Relic: Philosopher's Stone

Target: v0.111.0 / 41cef1ea, audited source
`Flaaax/STS2-source@706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20`.

Source: [`PhilosophersStone.cs`](https://github.com/Flaaax/STS2-source/blob/706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20/src/Core/Models/Relics/PhilosophersStone.cs).

Philosopher's Stone is Ancient. It adds 1 energy and applies 1 Strength to
opposing creatures when they enter combat. The emulator models the energy
bonus and applies Strength to all enemies at combat start; this update adds
the relic to the boss reward pool.

The source also handles opponents spawned into a combat after it starts; that
late-spawn hook is not modeled. Boss reward RNG parity remains unverified.
