# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 056 — Lost Wisp

Pinned `LostWisp.cs` is an Event relic offered by the Act 2 Hive Lost Wisp
event. After its owner plays a Power card during combat, Lost Wisp deals 8
unpowered damage to every hittable enemy. The Claim choice adds Decay and
grants the relic.

The emulator's combat trigger requires a played Power card and targets all
living enemies. Coverage ties that behavior to the Hive Claim option and
keeps the Event relic out of ordinary rarity draws.
