# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 047 — Shadowmeld and Wraith Form

This pass promotes Shadowmeld and Wraith Form and introduces two reusable modifier lifecycles.

## Shadowmeld

Shadowmeld costs 1 (0) and applies one Shadowmeld counter for the current player turn. Each stack
doubles all Block gained by that player, after card-only Dexterity/Frail adjustments. Multiple
stacks compose multiplicatively. The power is removed at player-turn end.

## Wraith Form

Wraith Form costs 3 and applies 2 (3) Intangible plus one Wraith Form debuff. Intangible caps
incoming combat damage/HP loss at 1 and decrements after each enemy turn. Wraith Form applies -1
Dexterity at each subsequent player-turn start.

Signed Dexterity applications are classified as debuffs for Artifact handling, matching the signed
Strength treatment introduced for Malaise. The Wraith Form debuff itself is also Artifact-blockable.
