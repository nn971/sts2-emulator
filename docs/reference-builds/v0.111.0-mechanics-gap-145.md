# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 145 — Art of War energy timing

The pinned Art of War tracks whether the owner played an Attack on each side
turn. After energy reset on turn 2 or later, it grants 1 energy if the previous
turn had no Attacks, then clears its turn flags. Its `AfterEnergyReset` hook
runs before the opening hand draw.

The emulator now resolves the bonus on `PlayerSideTurnStarted`, which its
automatic pipeline dispatches immediately after refreshing energy and before
drawing the hand. The trigger requires turn 2 or later and zero Attacks played
last turn. It uses the existing turn counter, so it does not reproduce the
source relic's internal status and attack-flag transitions. No new executable
coverage claim is recorded.
