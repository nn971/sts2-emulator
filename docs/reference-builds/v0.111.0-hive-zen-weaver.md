# Hive Zen Weaver

Pinned source: `Flaaax/STS2-source@706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20`,
[`ZenWeaver.cs`](https://github.com/Flaaax/STS2-source/blob/706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20/src/Core/Models/Events/ZenWeaver.cs).

Zen Weaver is offered only when the player has at least 125 gold. Its three
options are:

| Choice | Cost | Effect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Breathing Techniques | 50 gold | Add two Enlightenment cards |
| Emotional Awareness | 125 gold | Remove one card, then pay |
| Arachnid Acupuncture | 250 gold | Remove up to two cards, then pay |

The two removal options charge gold after card selection. Every option calls
`SetEventFinished` after its effects settle. The emulator keeps the selected
result page available until the player continues to the map.

The event registration requires 125 gold; the individual option prices control
which choices can be selected. The three-act route includes it in Hive.
