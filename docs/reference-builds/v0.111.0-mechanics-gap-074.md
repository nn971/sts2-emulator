# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 074 — Whispering Earring

Whispering Earring is offered in Vakuu's first Glory relic pool and adds one
Energy each turn. On turn one, it repeatedly auto-plays the first playable
hand card, paying its current cost, until 13 cards have played, no card is
playable, or Energy runs out.

Executable coverage dispatches three Silent cards through the normal play
pipeline. It verifies their effects, Energy cost, first-turn gating, and that
a melted copy does nothing. Normal Ancient assignment and native RNG parity
remain unverified.
