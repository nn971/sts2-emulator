# v0.111.0 Shared Relic: Runic Pyramid

Target: v0.111.0 / 41cef1ea, audited source
`Flaaax/STS2-source@706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20`.

Source: [`RunicPyramid.cs`](https://github.com/Flaaax/STS2-source/blob/706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20/src/Core/Models/Relics/RunicPyramid.cs).

Runic Pyramid is Ancient. Its owner does not flush their hand at the end of a
turn. The emulator models this through `PreventsHandDiscard` and its hand
flush path; this update adds the relic to the boss relic reward pool.

Ethereal cards still leave play during hand flush, matching the separate
Ethereal handling in the emulator. Boss reward RNG parity remains unverified.
