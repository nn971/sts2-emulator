The probe records history entries at intermediate mutation points,
not a fully aligned replayable decision transcript. Values below are
**directly observed event outcomes**, not proof that the emulator can
start from the native seed and reproduce all intermediary RNG events.

## Combat #5: Phrog Parasite elite

- Initial native parasite: **66 HP**, `InfestedPower` applied
  with amount **4**. Round 1: `INFECT_MOVE` generated **three
  Infection** statuses without damaging the player.
- Round 2: Player had **41 HP** before the Lash and **10 block**.
  Native `LASH_MOVE` landed **four 5-damage hits** (10 blocked,
  10 unblocked), leaving player at **31 HP**.
- Round 3: `INFECT_MOVE` added three more Infection cards.
- Round 4: `LASH_MOVE` again; this trace includes additional
  player damage at the player-turn-end boundary, so do not
  misattribute all HP loss in this round to Lash.
- Round 5: Killing the parasite brought four stunned Wrigglers
  into battle (`wriggler1` through `wriggler4`); their first
  enemy actions are `SPAWNED_MOVE` (stun), then subsequent
  numbered-slot initial intents. See issue #6 fix.

The corresponding isolated A10 emulator regression verifies
**Infect → Lash**, the three generated Infection cards,
and the **10 block + 10 HP** result. It deliberately does
not assert complete round-4/round-5 parity.

### Definite discrepancy fixed: current-card Slow increment

Pinned source `Flaaax/STS2-source@706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20`,
`src/Core/Models/Powers/SlowPower.cs`:
`SlowAmount` increases in `AfterCardPlayed`, *after*
the current card's damage has been computed. Thus an Attack
uses the number of **previously completed** card plays
only. The old emulator incorrectly added `+1` for the
attack resolving. This inflated attacks on the Slow enemy;
e.g., Dagger Spray played as the third card would give
5+5 instead of the native 4+4.

The emulator now uses `CardsPlayedThisTurn` alone for
Slow's incoming-attack multiplier in both standard damage
and repeat/echo damage paths. Direct tests check A10
Dagger Spray totals for the **first, third and fourth**
played-card positions: **8, 8, 10** damage. Existing
tests are updated to source-correct prior-play semantics.

Another isolated A10 regression checks the Effigy's
**25 → 18 through Weak → 1 HP through 17 block**.
Further action-level differential testing should inspect
card-generated powers, damage timing, and player turn
hand transitions, not just initial move lists.

## Remaining comparison requirements

A full native-vs-emulator action-state diff still needs
a faithful bridge between native card-instance identities,
observable hand contents, player decisions/targets,
and emulator state injection with an explicit RNG policy.
This source document and tests do not claim that bridge
exists or that unobserved transitions match.
