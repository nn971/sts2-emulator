Source: [`EternalFeather.cs`](https://github.com/Flaaax/STS2-source/blob/706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20/src/Core/Models/Relics/EternalFeather.cs).

Eternal Feather is an Uncommon shared relic. On entering a Rest Site room,
it heals 3 HP for every complete five cards in the deck, whether the player
later heals, trains, or smiths. The emulator applies this during map-room
entry and caps healing at Max HP. It is registered in the Silent shared
relic pool and uncommon rarity queue.

Native relic-pool generation and route fidelity remain unverified.
