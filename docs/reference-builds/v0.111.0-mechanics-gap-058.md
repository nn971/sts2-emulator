# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 058 — Shared boss relic reward integration

The configured Silent route uses a separate boss relic offer pool. Four pinned
shared relics had executable mechanics and appeared in that pool, but the
coverage report did not connect their relic records to boss rewards. Coverage
now verifies pool membership and each source-backed mechanic:

- Astrolabe transforms three selected cards and upgrades the results.
- Sozu grants one energy per turn and prevents potion acquisition.
- Snecko Eye draws two additional cards and randomizes drawn card costs over
  the four native cost outcomes.
- Velvet Choker grants one energy per turn and limits the player to six card
  plays per turn.
