The pinned `TheKinBoss.GenerateMonsters()` explicitly marks the **first**
KinFollower (`slot1`) as `StartsWithDance=true` and constructs the
other follower second, followed by the priest. The legacy prototype
registered a different model/slot order, `quick` and `dance`. The
experimental native entry replaces that *formation*, assigns CombatIds
in actual native source creation order, and supplies the legacy AI
conditional only with temporary `slot1 -> dance` and
`slot2 -> quick` aliases while preserving native slots in serialized
state. No extra random draw is introduced.

## RNG ownership and constraints

The opt-in `NativeActOneEliteBossCombatEntryV111.Prepare` requires:

- configured persistent native run RNG;
- exact Act 1 Overgrowth/Underdocks profile and region;
- source-shaped Act 1 map coordinates, with boss floor 16 and elite
  floors before 16, and the correct room type;
- one of the twelve explicit pinned native encounter identities.

Combat creation and starting intents use persistent `run.niche`
and `run.monster_ai` and independently seeded monster-local streams.
The independent encounter RNG remains at cursor 0. Every monster
entry is source-ordered; native CombatIds, initial HP and intentions
are snapshot/fork safe. Unsupported model identity and wrong-region
rooms fail loudly without prototype formation fallback.

The default whole-run prototype and parent AI training remain
unchanged.

## Deliberate fidelity boundary

This change covers **initial combat formation and its RNG ownership**,
not complete elite/boss behavior. The existing prototype remains
responsible for model/phase combat effects, card draws, relic hooks,
intent rule implementations, encounter selection, rewards, and act
completion; their native RNG/call-order parity has not been certified.
In particular, native encounter bag selection and the
multi-creature boss lifecycle still need source/trace auditing.

No live-game differential oracle trace exists in this workspace. Tests
compare source-audited constructions against the emulator and check
deterministic state invariants, not full native gameplay parity.

## Regression suite
