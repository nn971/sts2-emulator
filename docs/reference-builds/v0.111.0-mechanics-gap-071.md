# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 071 — Beautiful Bracelet

Beautiful Bracelet is an Ancient relic offered by Nonupeipe in Glory when the
deck has at least four cards that can receive Swift. Its acquisition applies
Swift 2 to four distinct eligible cards.

Executable coverage creates an eligible five-card Silent deck and checks a
deterministic Nonupeipe offer containing Beautiful Bracelet. A three-card deck
omits it. The acquisition helper enchants four of the five cards and preserves
the fifth. Normal Ancient assignment and native RNG parity remain unverified.
