# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 078 — Storybook and Brightest Flame

Storybook is a Tezcatara Ancient reward. Acquiring it adds the event-only
Brightest Flame Skill to the deck. Brightest Flame grants two Energy, draws two
cards and loses two Max HP; its upgrade raises Energy and draw to three without
adding Innate.

Executable coverage follows the Tezcatara reward through the relic-acquisition
hook, checks that a melted copy adds no card, and verifies the event-only card's
base and upgraded definition. The behavior is not native replay verified.
