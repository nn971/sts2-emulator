# v0.111.0 mechanics gap 081 — Seal of Gold

Seal of Gold is an Act 2 Tezcatara reward. At each player-side turn start, it
grants one Energy and spends three Gold if the player can afford the cost.

Executable coverage checks the Tezcatara reward and trigger, the two-Gold and
three-Gold boundary, duplicate copies with five and six Gold, a melted copy,
and a melted copy before an active duplicate. Each purchase evaluates the
remaining Gold when its ordered trigger runs. Native Ancient assignment and
RNG parity remain unverified.
