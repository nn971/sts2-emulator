# Underdocks: first native-shaped single-player slice (v0.111.0)

This is **an opt-in partial Act 1 region**, not a claim that
Underdocks can be completed in the emulator yet.

Source pin: `Flaaax/STS2-source@706ef1c9fa2219220065849fd5265328607ece20`:
`src/Core/Models/Acts/Underdocks.cs`,
`src/Core/Models/Encounters/{SeapunkWeak,SludgeSpinnerWeak,ToadpolesWeak,CorpseSlugsWeak,CorpseSlugsNormal,PunchConstructNormal,CultistsNormal,FossilStalkerNormal,SeapunkNormal,HauntedShipNormal,SewerClamNormal,LivingFogNormal,TwoTailedRatsNormal,GremlinMercNormal,SkulkingColonyElite,PhantasmalGardenersElite,TerrorEelElite,SoulFyshBoss,LagavulinMatriarchBoss,WaterfallGiantBoss}.cs`
and the corresponding monster models.

## Activation

`PrototypeNativeUnderdocksRunFactory.Create(seed, ascension)` produces the
native-shaped Silent Act 1 opening with the shared Neow event, starter deck,
Ascension adjustments, a 15-room/16-floor map and the distinct
`native-underdocks-map-structure-v0.111.0-v1` map profile.

The existing Overgrowth factory and ordinary `PrototypeGameFactory` reset
remain unchanged. Underdocks is **not** the default run mode.

## Source inventory (20 encounters)

| Room | Number | Native inventory | Implemented |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Weak ordinary | 4 | Corpse Slugs, Seapunk, Sludge Spinner, Toadpoles | **4 / 4** |
| Normal ordinary | 10 | Corpse Slugs, Cultists, Fossil Stalker, Gremlin Merc, Haunted Ship, Living Fog, Punch Construct, Seapunk, Sewer Clam, Two-Tailed Rats | **10 / 10** (all native normal encounters) |
| Elite | 3 | Phantasmal Gardeners, Skulking Colony, Terror Eel | **3 / 3** (Skulking Colony, Phantasmal Gardeners, Terror Eel) |
| Boss | 3 | Waterfall Giant, Soul Fysh, Lagavulin Matriarch | **3 / 3** (Soul Fysh, Lagavulin Matriarch, Waterfall Giant) |

The initial **three** ordinary combats draw without replacement from
all **four** pinned Underdocks weak encounters. Corpse Slugs has a two-monster
formation with different initial moves, one shared initial-offset roll,
and a three-move cycle. Ravenous is a source-backed ally-death reaction:
when another ally dies, the living slug gains Strength equal to its Ravenous
stacks and is stunned for one upcoming enemy turn. This is resolved from
both attack and non-attack HP-loss paths; exact intent RNG and visualization
of the native stun animation remain outside the fidelity scope.

Seapunk and Toadpole already have native-shaped move models in the shared
catalog; the initial slice improved Seapunk's A8 block and A9 Strength values,
added Sludge Spinner's 37–39 HP (A8: 41–42), forced Oil Spray,
no-repeat weighted continuation (Oil Spray, Slam, Rage), Weak and Strength,
and registered front/back Toadpoles. The Corpse Slug expansion adds its
25–27 HP (A8: 27–29), Ravenous Strength 4 (A9: 5), cyclic Whip Slap /
Glomp / Goop and paired staggered openers.

After the three weak combats, a separate normal encounter bag draws **without replacement**
from **all ten** pinned normals: Corpse Slugs Normal (three
slugs with staggered Whip Slap / Glomp / Goop opening states), Punch
Construct Normal (one construct, Artifact 1, its ready → fast punch → strong
punch cycle), Cultists Normal (Calcified and Damp Cultist), Fossil
Stalker Normal (one stalker), Seapunk Normal (Calcified Cultist + Seapunk),
Haunted Ship Normal (one ship), Sewer Clam Normal (one clam) and Living
Fog Normal (one fog with summoned Gas Bombs), and Two-Tailed Rats
Normal (three rats with staggered starters and conditional backup calls),
and Gremlin Merc Normal (a single merc whose death spawns two gremlins). This is a deliberately restricted training pool, **not**
a claim about native encounter probabilities. When all ten have been consumed,
the next ordinary fight fails explicitly; the engine does not borrow an
Overgrowth encounter or silently refill the restricted bag.

Cultists use pinned Incantation values (Calcified Ritual 2; Damp Ritual
5, or 6 at A9) followed by repeated Dark Strike. The shared Ritual power
tracks whether its enemy owner applied it on the current side turn; it does
not grant Strength on that first turn, and grants the full Ritual amount at
each subsequent enemy side-turn end (including a stunned turn).

Fossil Stalker opens with Latch, then chooses Tackle, Latch or double-hit
Lash with equal branch weights. Its starting Suck 3 power grants Strength
per powered attack hit that deals unblocked HP damage; fully blocked hits
and non-attack HP loss do not contribute. These behaviors are generic
power hooks, not encounter-ID special cases.

Seapunk Normal reuses two complete existing enemy models with a fixed
Calcified Cultist / Seapunk formation. Haunted Ship Normal adds the source
opening Haunt (3 Weak and five Ethereal Dazed cards to the discard pile),
then cycles Swipe / Stomp / Swipe. It uses the shared status-card creation,
player-power application and deterministic enemy state-machine machinery;
no character-specific effects or implicit substitutes are required.

Sewer Clam begins with Block equal to Plating (8; A8: 9), opens with Jet,
and alternates Pressurize (+4 Strength) and Jet. Plating grants Block at
enemy-side turn end and decays by one at the start of enemy-side turns
after the first. These stage hooks are reusable power attributes.

Living Fog's Advanced Gas gives Smoggy, which afflicts all eligible Skill
cards after a Skill is played. Such cards are unplayable until Smog clears
at the player's turn end. Bloat summons one Gas Bomb in the last unoccupied
bomb slot (of five), then attacks; Gas Bombs skip their summoning turn and
explode for damage before killing themselves on the next turn. All of these
behaviors are declared on the enemy, power and summon specs rather than
hardcoded to one encounter ID.

Two-Tailed Rats Normal starts three rats in the third, fourth and fifth
slots with three distinct opening moves. Each rat must use two non-summoning
moves before Call for Backup can become eligible. Thereafter the source
weighted branch is 1:1:3:9 (Scratch, Disease Bite, Screech, Backup), with
immediate-repeat restrictions on ordinary moves and once-per-rat
backup selection. Backup calls share a global three-call budget, require
a free named slot and reserve the remainder of that enemy turn for
the squad, preventing simultaneous duplicate calls. Newly summoned rats
do not act on their summoning turn. Exact native intent display/RNG
parity remains deferred.

Gremlin Merc starts alone with Surprise and Thievery 20, attacks and steals
up to 20 actual player gold after each move (not after each hit). On its
death, the encounter spawns Fat and Sneaky Gremlins. Fat inherits carried
gold and flees after its initial stunned/spawned move; killing Fat returns
carried gold as a separate claimable reward. If Fat escapes, regular gold
is reduced to half if nothing was stolen and zero if gold was stolen.
The Underdocks legacy base reward amount is still an approximation; only
the source-backed reward **proportions** and transferred gold are modeled.

Skulking Colony is an explicitly supported elite: a solo enemy with
75 HP (80 at A8), Hardened Shell 20 and four moves Zoom, Zoom, Inertia,
Piercing Stabs. Hardened Shell limits cumulative enemy HP loss to 20 per
enemy side turn, with its budget reset at the start of the following enemy
side turn. Elites are selected from all three implemented source-backed encounters.

Phantasmal Gardeners adds a fixed quartet (first, second, third, fourth)
with one distinct initial move each: Flail, Bite, Lash, Enlarge.
Thereafter every Gardener cycles Bite -> Lash -> Flail -> Enlarge.
Each starts with Skittish 6 (7 at A8), gaining that much Block after
the first unblocked player card attack against it each player side turn.
This once-per-turn defensive reaction is a shared power hook; it resets
after the owner's enemy turn. Enlarge adds Strength 2 (3 at A9).

Terror Eel starts with Shriek 70 (75 at A8), opening Crash (16, A9 18)
then Thrash (3x3, A9 4x3) and Vigor 6. Vigor adds 6 to all hits of the
next attack command and expires after that command. Once the eel receives
unblocked damage bringing it to or below the Shriek threshold, Shriek is
removed and its next action is Stun, then Terror (99 Vulnerable), before
resuming Crash/Thrash. These use generic HP-threshold AI redirection and
post-attack power consumption rather than encounter-ID checks.

Soul Fysh is the first supported Underdocks boss. Its loop is Beckon →
De-Gas → Gaze → Fade → Scream. Beckon inserts one status randomly into the
draw pile and one into discard, while Gaze adds one more to discard.
Beckon costs one energy and causes six unblockable HP loss at player turn
end if held. Fade grants Intangible for two enemy side-turn ends; during
that window enemy HP loss is limited to one per damage event. Scream deals
13 (A9: 15) damage and applies 3 Vulnerable. This is an initial functional
implementation, not native RNG-sequence parity or live oracle validation.

Lagavulin Matriarch begins with Plating 12 and Asleep 3. An unblocked
player attack removes both powers, redirects its AI to Slash and stuns
the pending enemy action. Otherwise, the boss sleeps for three turns;
the last sleep removes Plating before the side-turn-end block stage and
prepares Slash. Its awake loop is Slash, Disembowel (two hits), Slash2
(attack and block), and Soul Siphon (player Strength/Dexterity -2, owner
Strength +2). Wake and power-expiry reactions are declarative rules,
not a Matriarch-specific combat branch.

Waterfall Giant cycles Pressurize → Stomp → Ram → Siphon →
Pressure Gun → Pressure Up → Stomp. Steam Eruption stacks are accrued
during the sequence; Siphon heals, and Pressure Gun deals five extra
damage for each prior use. When it would be killed while carrying Steam
Eruption, a generic last-stand hook replaces the lethal blow with a
protected scripted telegraph. About to Blow snapshots the accumulated
Steam Eruption stacks as delayed damage and removes the power; the
following Explode attack deals that damage and self-destructs. The
combat must not end on the first apparent lethal hit.

## Underdocks event implementation (initial slice)

The native list contains Abyssal Baths, Drowning Beacon, Endless Conveyor,
Punch Off, Spiraling Whirlpool, Sunken Statue, Sunken Treasury, Doors of Light
and Dark, Trash Heap and Waterlogged Scriptorium. **Sunken Treasury**,
**Doors of Light and Dark**, **Sunken Statue**, **Abyssal Baths**,
**Spiraling Whirlpool**, **Drowning Beacon**, and **Trash Heap** are
currently enabled.

Sunken Treasury stores its two independent source-defined rolls when the
event is selected: first chest gains 52–67 gold, second gains 303–363 gold
and adds the unplayable Greed curse. The unchosen chest never alters the RNG.
Doors of Light and Dark upgrades up to two distinct upgradable deck cards
at random or offers one player-selected removal through the existing event
deck-choice machinery. Underdocks event nodes never silently draw Overgrowth
or generic prototype events.

Sunken Statue offers the source-backed Sword of Stone relic or 101–121
rolled gold at the cost of 7 unblocked HP. The gold value is rolled
and stored at event entry; selecting the sword does not grant gold or
consume a second random draw.

Abyssal Baths now implements its native multi-page sequence: Abstain heals 10;
the initial Immerse and each subsequent Linger first gain 2 maximum/current
HP and then take 3, 4, 5, ... unblocked damage respectively. Exit ends
the event without further effects. Immersions can continue beyond the
native ninth descriptive Linger page, and lethal Linger remains a valid
decision. The immersion count is held in forkable event state, with no
event RNG rolls. Native death-warning *text* is UI-only and not simulated.

Spiraling Whirlpool is now source-backed. Event selection requires at
least one enchantable basic **Strike**- or **Defend**-tagged deck card,
following `Spiral.CanEnchant`. Observe prompts the player to select
exactly one eligible card and persistently applies Spiral +1 through the
shared event enchantment continuation; Spiral's additional card execution
is already modeled in combat. Drink heals `floor(MaxHP × 0.33)`, capped
at current maximum HP. These choices require no additional event RNG;
eligible-card filtering is enforced both during event selection and when
constructing the deck prompt. Native reward and card selection UI are
outside the canonical state scope.

Drowning Beacon's Bottle now offers the native event-only **Glowwater
Potion** through an optional take/skip/replace-potion continuation (including
an empty belt slot). Glowwater exhausts the entire current hand and then
draws up to ten cards through the ordinary shuffle, card-draw and hand-limit
machinery; exhaust events dispatch in hand order, with supported
suspendable subscribers. Climb loses **13 max HP** before granting
**Fresnel Lens**. The relic grants persistent **Nimble +2** to otherwise
unenhanted, newly acquired cards with modelled Block gain effects,
including card rewards, shops and other ordinary `AppendCard` acquisitions.
Nimble adds +2 before the existing card-Block multipliers and follows
the selected card into combat. Cards already in the deck at acquisition
remain unchanged. The native relic also modifies **displayed reward/shop
offers before acquisition**; that early option-preview decoration is
still pending, whereas the obtained card carries the enchantment.
The emulator's `GainsBlock` eligibility currently uses typed card Block
effects and needs comparison with the native card metadata where abilities
gain Block indirectly. This slice claims functional mechanics rather than
exact native event/RNG sequencing.

Trash Heap follows the pinned `TrashHeap.cs` event identity and choice order.
It appears only above **5 current HP**. Grab immediately grants **100 gold**
and uniformly draws one card from the exact source list (Caltrops, Clash,
Distraction, Dual Wield, Entrench, Hello World, Outmaneuver, Rebound,
Rip and Tear, Stack). Dive removes **8 unblocked HP** and only rolls its
uniform five-relic list on survival (Darkstone Periapt, Dream Catcher,
Hand Drill, Maw Bank, The Boot). In particular, HP 6–8 still permits Dive,
and a lethal choice consumes *no* item RNG. Both lists exclude synthetic
Silent/card/relic substitutes and the extra event items are excluded from
ordinary reward generation. Caltrops, Clash, Entrench, Outmaneuver,
Rip and Tear, and Stack have typed executable combat effects, including
Clash's hand-only-Attacks condition and Stack's discard-pile scaling.
The remaining four cards (Distraction, Dual Wield, Hello World, Rebound)
are catalogued with **MechanicsImplemented=false**, so attempting to play
one fails explicitly. The four newly introduced relics are catalogued,
but Dream Catcher rest rewards, Hand Drill block-break triggers, Maw Bank
paid-purchase state, and The Boot damage threshold are not yet implemented.
A duplicate randomly selected Trash Heap relic fails explicitly until
native duplicate `RelicCmd.Obtain` behavior is verified; it is never rerolled
to disguise the source distribution. Thus the event has *correct identities,
eligibility, branch order and candidate probabilities*, but complete
run-mechanics parity is **not** claimed yet.

## Explicitly unsupported / next steps

- Exhausted normal and elite encounter bags still fail explicitly instead
  of substituting unrelated content. All three native bosses are spawnable,
  with the selected boss preserved without substitutions.
- The first **seven of ten** Underdocks-specific events are wired to a
  distinct region-specific source-backed pool; the other three events remain
  explicitly excluded. Native Underdocks event weights/RNG parity are not yet
  claimed. No Overgrowth events or generic prototype events appear on
  opt-in Underdocks event nodes.
- Rewards, merchants and act transitions still follow the applicable
  prototype/native-shaped shared rules, which are not a claim of full
  native Underdocks semantics.
- The map shares the same geometry approximation as Overgrowth because both
  pinned Act 1 source classes specify `BaseNumberOfRooms=15`,
  `NumberOfWeakEncounters=3`, and the same map-point distributions.
  Exact native RNG seed parity remains a separate track.

**Recommended next work:** finish the six catalogued Trash Heap
item mechanics, then expand the remaining three Underdocks native
events (Endless Conveyor, Punch Off and Waterlogged Scriptorium)
and their source-specific gating, then run broader native Act 1
smoke/fidelity coverage. Source probability distributions and comprehensive oracle
differentials remain separate fidelity work.

## Regression surface

`PrototypeUnderdocksOpeningTests` verifies the native inventory, opt-in
initialization, deterministic ten-normal bag depletion and exhaustion,
triple-slug staggered openers, Punch Construct’s existing model and Artifact,
no leak from Overgrowth, four weak encounter coverage, unsupported room gating,
canonical state forks and unchanged default Overgrowth reset. Separate
`PrototypeCorpseSlugsTests` verifies Ravenous, poison death and cyclic opens.
`PrototypeUnderdocksCultistsAndFossilTests` checks pinned HP/Ascension values,
Ritual first-side-turn suppression followed by Strength gains, and Suck's
unblocked hit-counting including double attacks and attacks fully absorbed by
player Block. `PrototypeUnderdocksSeapunkHauntedShipTests` covers
native formations, Haunted Ship Dazed/Weak opener, deterministic Swipe/Stomp
cycling, Ascension HP/damage, and Seapunk/Cultist independent move and
Ritual progression. `PrototypeUnderdocksClamFogTests` verifies staged
Plating decay/block gains, Living Fog's Smoggy Skill restriction, fixed
Gas Bomb slot order and turn-delayed explosive minion lifecycle.
`PrototypeUnderdocksRatsTests` verifies the staggered starting moves,
source-weighted branching, cooldown constraints, summon slot ordering
and shared squad call-count propagation. `PrototypeUnderdocksMercTests`
verifies gold stolen once per attack command, death-triggered transfer to
Fat Gremlin, delayed escape, and recovery as an optional gold reward.
`PrototypeUnderdocksSkulkingColonyTests` verifies the elite's four-turn
cycle and Hardened Shell HP-loss limit resetting on the enemy side turn.
`PrototypeUnderdocksGardenersTests` verifies quartet slot openers, source-backed
HP/Ascension scaling, repeated move cycle and reactive Skittish Block.
`PrototypeUnderdocksTerrorEelTests` verifies the Shriek HP threshold
stun/Terror redirect, Ascension scaling and Vigor consumed after its next attack.
`PrototypeUnderdocksSoulFyshTests` validates source-backed boss stats,
Beckon status insertion and unblocked end-turn HP loss, five-move rotation,
expiring enemy Intangible and identity-preserving boss selection.
`PrototypeUnderdocksMatriarchTests` covers normal sleep expiration, early
unblocked wake, Plating timing, and the awake attack/buff sequence.
`PrototypeUnderdocksWaterfallGiantTests` covers pressure escalation, healing,
Steam Eruption accumulation and two-stage lethal interception/explosion.

`PrototypeNativeUnderdocksEventsTests` covers regional event isolation,
independent Treasury rolls, Greed creation, Doors random upgrades/removal,
Statue's exclusive Sword of Stone or persisted gold-for-HP outcome, and
Abyssal Baths' repeating/fatal immersion lifecycle, and Spiraling
Whirlpool's eligible deck enchantment, persistent Spiral, and percentage
healing. `PrototypeUnderdocksDrowningBeaconTests` covers the Drowning
Beacon potion offer, full-belt replacement, max HP tradeoff, Nimble
applied when acquiring Backflip, and the Glowwater exhaust-then-draw
combat sequence. `PrototypeUnderdocksTrashHeapTests` covers the pinned
item catalogs, source-specific probability rolls, low-HP eligibility,
lethal-dive RNG ordering and unchanged normal reward pool.

These are lightweight functionality tests, **not** live oracle differentials.
