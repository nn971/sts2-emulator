## Source behavior now represented

Knowledge Demon's precommitted `curse_of_knowledge` enemy move
stops at a player-owned decision boundary. Its three subsequent offers
are exactly:

| Curse move use | Choice one | Choice two |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First | Disintegration (6 damage) | Mind Rot (draw -1 per player turn) |
| Second | Disintegration (7 damage) | Sloth (max 3 cards played per turn) |
| Third | Disintegration (8 damage) | Waste Away (max energy -1) |

The displayed status cards implement source `IChoosable.OnChosen`: their
selection immediately applies a debuff power to the player, and the cards
are **never inserted** into the combat deck, discard or persistent deck.
The choice remains visible as two explicit legal
`choose_knowledge_curse` actions; no automatically selected fallback.

A `PrototypeKnowledgeDemonChoiceState` records enemy instance identity,
the originating side-turn, the zero-based curse stage and the two offered
IDs. The engine stores this in `CombatState` plus the normal automatic
step continuation, and resumes the *same* enemy-action step only after the
player's choice is applied. The normal enemy AI move-use counter is updated
by the actual move exactly once, so the three scripted curse uses lead into
the final Slap branch without a fourth offer.

- Disintegration is unpowered, blockable damage at player-side turn end.
- Mind Rot decreases hand draw, with final hand size clamped at zero.
- Sloth uses the existing per-turn cap shared by all card-play paths.
- Waste Away reduces refreshed energy and clamps the result at zero.
- Choices, RNG and combat state support fork/exact snapshot/restore;
  the source status models are excluded from normal generation/rewards.
- Malformed stage/option/turn/enemy IDs or an unavailable foreign choice
  fail closed; explicit tests exercise all three offers.

## Remaining fidelity caveats

This is an integration implementation, not a game-oracle matched sequence.
Native `AfterSideTurnEndLate` hook ordering, active power interactions,
enemy-driven zero-draw behavior, identical RNG stream ownership and
status-bar visual models require differential reference traces.
Ancients and other regional event/boss interactions remain outside
this particular patch.
