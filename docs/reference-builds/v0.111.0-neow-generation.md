## Implemented offer construction

A default, unmodified single-player Neow opening presents **two positive
relic options and one cursed option**. The native selection graph chooses
the cursed relic first, then applies a conflict exclusion to the positive
pool:

- Cursed Pearl removes Golden Pearl.
- Hefty Tablet removes Arcane Scroll.
- Leafy Poultice removes New Leaf.
- Precarious Shears removes Precise Scissors.
- Neow's Sacrifice removes Phial Holster and Lost Coffer.

The fourteen core positive choices are extended by three randomized
binary groups. Lava Rock / Small Capsule is suppressed entirely if the
curse is Large Capsule. Nutritious Oyster / Stone Humidifier and
Neow's Talisman / Pomander are each added as one of two choices.
The multiplayer-only Massive Scroll is removed by the native single-player
eligibility check, but retained in the reference catalog. Two eligible positives
are then chosen without replacement and the curse is appended third.

The emulator mirrors this *selection structure*, registering all
30 possible relic identities and validating pair exclusions over a
large deterministic seed sweep. It still uses `PrototypeRng`, so
identical native seed values **do not guarantee identical Neow choices**.
Other native `IsAllowedAtNeow` conditions and unlock-state filtering remain to be
translated. Modifier-provided Neow options are outside the current
single-player A0 unmodified-run target.

## Implemented effect subset

The following native behaviors now affect canonical persistent state:

- **Nutritious Oyster:** +11 max HP and HP upon acquisition.
- **Golden Pearl:** +150 gold upon acquisition.
- **Arcane Scroll:** acquire exactly one Rare card at Neow, using the
  prototype Silent reward pool and reward RNG stream in place of the
  native uniform Rare-card creation/RNG machinery.
- **Neow's Talisman:** upgrade the final Strike and final Defend in
  the persistent starting deck (source order), with a generic
  last-matching-card run effect.
- **Pomander:** one explicit eligible deck-card upgrade choice,
  suspended and resolved before the first map selection.
- **Cursed Pearl:** add the Eternal, unplayable Greed curse to the
  persistent deck and gain 333 gold.
- **Stone Humidifier:** +5 maximum HP and HP after each rest-site heal
  (not after training or card upgrading), via a reusable
  `RestSiteHealed` run trigger.
- **Precise Scissors:** remove one selected persistent deck card in a
  suspended Neow choice before map selection.
- **New Leaf:** transform one selected persistent deck card in the same
  continuation; its replacement currently uses the prototype transformation pool.
- **Silken Tress:** lose all starting gold upon relic acquisition. Its later
  one-shot Glam card-reward enchantment still requires native reward hooks.

Other Neow relics are still metadata-only or have incomplete native
effects. Choosing one may therefore produce a non-native strategic
state even though the generator and transition remain executable.

## Further source-shaped relics and quests

- **Precarious Shears:** offers a two-card persistent-deck removal
  continuation and loses 16 HP. The native HP damage follows card
  selection; the emulator currently applies that loss immediately before
  the continuation, yielding the same post-Neow A0 result.
- **Leafy Poultice:** loses 12 maximum HP and transforms the first
  persistent Strike and first Defend in deck order. Replacements use the
  emulator's generic transform pool/RNG, preserving the selected
  persistent instance IDs.
- **Neow's Torment:** acquires Neow's Fury, a 1-cost 10 (14)-damage
  Exhaust Attack that may recover up to 2 (3) cards from discard.
  Current retrieval uses the existing choice continuation; native
  maximum-hand-size behavior requires a later cross-check.
- **Dowsing Rod:** acquires the unplayable Dowsing Quest card. Each entry
  into a *visible* question-mark map node increments the card's
  persistent count, even if the resolved room is a combat, shop,
  treasure, or event. On the fifth entry it becomes Abundance in place,
  preserving the deck instance identity. Abundance exhausts to choose
  one of three generated, upgraded Powers for free use that turn.
  The prototype reward pool and RNG stand in for native unlocked-card
  generation; the generated choice itself is mandatory.

The Neow pool remains opt-in and source-shaped. Several other
relics still need acquisition effects, counters, and ongoing hooks.
Selection and generated-card RNG are not yet native-call-order-parity
implementations.

## Validation and remaining work

Tests check complete option registration, repeatability, three
distinct offers, the multiplayer-only Massive Scroll exclusion, curse
incompatibilities, conditional mutually exclusive pairs, coverage across
seeds, acquisition effects, Pomander/Precise Scissors/New Leaf deck choices,
and the Stone Humidifier rest-heal lifecycle.

This work advances native Act 1 *generation* without claiming complete
native Neow effects, map RNG call order, event page trees, or
shops/rewards/rest-site economy. Do not begin the requested final
fidelity test until the remaining generation gaps are addressed.
