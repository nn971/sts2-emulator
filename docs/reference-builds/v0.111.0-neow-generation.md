## Implemented offer construction

A default, unmodified single-player Neow opening presents **two positive
relic options and one cursed option**. The native selection graph chooses
the cursed relic first, then applies a conflict exclusion to the positive
pool:

- Cursed Pearl removes Golden Pearl.
- Hefty Tablet removes Arcane Scroll.
- Leafy Poultice removes New Leaf.
- Precarious Shears removes Precise Scissors.
- Neow's Sacrifice removes Phial Holster and Lost Coffer.

The fourteen core positive choices are extended by three randomized
binary groups. Lava Rock / Small Capsule is suppressed entirely if the
curse is Large Capsule. Nutritious Oyster / Stone Humidifier and
Neow's Talisman / Pomander are each added as one of two choices.
Two positives are then chosen without replacement and the curse is
appended third.

The emulator mirrors this *selection structure*, registering all
30 possible relic identities and validating pair exclusions over a
large deterministic seed sweep. It still uses `PrototypeRng`, so
identical native seed values **do not guarantee identical Neow choices**.
Native `IsAllowedAtNeow` and unlock-state filtering remain to be
translated. Modifier-provided Neow options are outside the current
single-player A0 unmodified-run target.

## Implemented effect subset

The following native behaviors now affect canonical persistent state:

- **Nutritious Oyster:** +11 max HP and HP upon acquisition.
- **Golden Pearl:** +150 gold upon acquisition.
- **Neow's Talisman:** upgrade the final Strike and final Defend in
  the persistent starting deck (source order), with a generic
  last-matching-card run effect.
- **Pomander:** one explicit eligible deck-card upgrade choice,
  suspended and resolved before the first map selection.
- **Cursed Pearl:** add the Eternal, unplayable Greed curse to the
  persistent deck and gain 333 gold.
- **Stone Humidifier:** +5 maximum HP and HP after each rest-site heal
  (not after training or card upgrading), via a reusable
  `RestSiteHealed` run trigger.

Other Neow relics are still metadata-only or have incomplete native
effects. Choosing one may therefore produce a non-native strategic
state even though the generator and transition remain executable.

## Validation and remaining work

Tests check complete option registration, repeatability, three
distinct offers, curse incompatibilities, conditional mutually
exclusive pairs, coverage across seeds, the five immediate
acquisition consequences, the Pomander continuation, and the
Stone Humidifier rest-heal lifecycle.

This work advances native Act 1 *generation* without claiming complete
native Neow effects, map RNG call order, event page trees, or
shops/rewards/rest-site economy. Do not begin the requested final
fidelity test until the remaining generation gaps are addressed.
