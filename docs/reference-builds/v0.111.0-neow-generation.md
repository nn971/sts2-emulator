## Implemented offer construction

A default, unmodified single-player Neow opening presents **two positive
relic options and one cursed option**. The native selection graph chooses
the cursed relic first, then applies a conflict exclusion to the positive
pool:

- Cursed Pearl removes Golden Pearl.
- Hefty Tablet removes Arcane Scroll.
- Leafy Poultice removes New Leaf.
- Precarious Shears removes Precise Scissors.
- Neow's Sacrifice removes Phial Holster and Lost Coffer.

The fourteen core positive choices are extended by three randomized
binary groups. Lava Rock / Small Capsule is suppressed entirely if the
curse is Large Capsule. Nutritious Oyster / Stone Humidifier and
Neow's Talisman / Pomander are each added as one of two choices.
The multiplayer-only Massive Scroll is removed by the native single-player
eligibility check, but retained in the reference catalog. Two eligible positives
are then chosen without replacement and the curse is appended third.

The emulator mirrors this *selection structure*, registering all
30 possible relic identities and validating pair exclusions over a
large deterministic seed sweep. It still uses `PrototypeRng`, so
identical native seed values **do not guarantee identical Neow choices**.
Other native `IsAllowedAtNeow` conditions and unlock-state filtering remain to be
translated. Modifier-provided Neow options are outside the current
single-player A0 unmodified-run target.

## Implemented effect subset

The following native behaviors now affect canonical persistent state:

- **Nutritious Oyster:** +11 max HP and HP upon acquisition.
- **Golden Pearl:** +150 gold upon acquisition.
- **Arcane Scroll:** acquire exactly one Rare card at Neow, using the
  prototype Silent reward pool and reward RNG stream in place of the
  native uniform Rare-card creation/RNG machinery.
- **Neow's Talisman:** upgrade the final Strike and final Defend in
  the persistent starting deck (source order), with a generic
  last-matching-card run effect.
- **Pomander:** one explicit eligible deck-card upgrade choice,
  suspended and resolved before the first map selection.
- **Cursed Pearl:** add the Eternal, unplayable Greed curse to the
  persistent deck and gain 333 gold.
- **Stone Humidifier:** +5 maximum HP and HP after each rest-site heal
  (not after training or card upgrading), via a reusable
  `RestSiteHealed` run trigger.
- **Precise Scissors:** remove one selected persistent deck card in a
  suspended Neow choice before map selection.
- **New Leaf:** transform one selected persistent deck card in the same
  continuation; its replacement currently uses the prototype transformation pool.
- **Silken Tress:** lose all starting gold upon relic acquisition. Its later
  one-shot Glam card-reward enchantment still requires native reward hooks.

Other Neow relics are still metadata-only or have incomplete native
effects. Choosing one may therefore produce a non-native strategic
state even though the generator and transition remain executable.

## Further source-shaped relics and quests

- **Precarious Shears:** offers a two-card persistent-deck removal
  continuation and loses 16 HP **only after both removal choices
  resolve**, matching the native ordering of intermediate decisions.
  This is still modeled as direct HP subtraction instead of a full
  native unpowered-damage command.
- **Leafy Poultice:** loses 12 maximum HP and transforms the first
  persistent Strike and first Defend in deck order. Replacements use the
  emulator's generic transform pool/RNG, preserving the selected
  persistent instance IDs.
- **Neow's Torment:** acquires Neow's Fury, a 1-cost 10 (14)-damage
  Exhaust Attack that may recover up to 2 (3) cards from discard.
  Retrieval uses the existing choice continuation and caps selections
  at the free capacity of the ten-card hand.
- **Dowsing Rod:** acquires the unplayable Dowsing Quest card. Each entry
  into a *visible* question-mark map node increments the card's
  persistent count, even if the resolved room is a combat, shop,
  treasure, or event. On the fifth entry it becomes Abundance in place,
  preserving the deck instance identity. Abundance exhausts to choose
  one of three generated, upgraded Powers for free use that turn.
  The prototype reward pool and RNG stand in for native unlocked-card
  generation; the generated choice itself is mandatory.

The Neow pool remains opt-in and source-shaped. Several other
relics still need acquisition effects, counters, and ongoing hooks.
Selection and generated-card RNG are not yet native-call-order-parity
implementations.

## Persistent relic, curse, potion and reward mechanics

- **Phial Holster:** adds one potion slot and creates two random
  potions at pickup. The capacity increase is included in canonical
  player state and the structural invariants; later potion purchases,
  replacements, and combat use share the enlarged inventory.
- **Large Capsule:** acquires two different random unowned relics
  and adds a new Silent Strike and Defend. The current implementation
  samples from the emulator's reward-eligible relic pool rather than
  the pinned native relic deck.
- **Neow's Sacrifice:** adds one Ambergris potion and the unplayable
  Guilty curse. Guilty persists its completed-combat counter and
  removes itself from the persistent deck after its fifth combat
  victory, including elite or boss fights. Ambergris is excluded
  from the ordinary potion pool; its 50%-of-maximum-HP heal is
  executable in combat and outside combat. The native **extra turn
  when used during combat** is not implemented yet.
- **Fishing Rod:** tracks ordinary monster-combat victories on the
  relic instance and upgrades an eligible persistent deck card
  every third such victory. The card choice uses the emulator RNG,
  rather than the native Niche stream.
- **Booming Conch:** first-turn Elite combat gets +2 card draws
  and +1 energy; normal and boss fights do not receive this bonus.
- **Silver Crucible:** the first three generated combat
  card-reward **groups** offer their cards upgraded. Both the offer
  and the associated upgrade metadata are stored explicitly in
  `RewardState`, and the relic counter advances when an offer
  is generated even if the player skips it. The first treasure
  room visited while holding Silver Crucible gives no relic; it
  is still counted as a completed room, and a subsequent treasure
  room generates a reward normally. Native non-combat custom
  card-reward groups are not yet wired into this counter.

These features run through the normal run-map, combat-win,
reward-choice, deck, and inventory systems. The full native
selection pools, reward rarity generation, card-specific upgrade
eligibility, and RNG stream fidelity remain separate tasks.

## Custom Neow reward continuations

The Neow opening can now suspend its event and enter the shared
typed `Reward` phase for native pickup rewards. The reward's
`SourceRoom` tracks its event provenance. After reward completion
the engine resumes the same event continuation rather than
recording a new map room or replaying acquisition effects.
Deferred rewards and card bundles are deep-forked in canonical
run state.

- **Hefty Tablet:** three distinct Silent Rare card options,
  optionally select one or skip; the unplayable Injury curse
  is appended **after** reward completion, even if the
  Rare card was skipped. Native unlock filters and RNG are
  still represented by the prototype pool.
- **Small Capsule:** one random unowned relic is offered
  through the normal relic reward action. Its relic acquisition
  and any deck-choice follow-up use the shared reward pipeline.
- **Lost Coffer:** one standard three-card offer plus one
  random potion reward, independently skippable. Full
  potion belts use the existing replacement actions.
- **Neow's Bones:** two independent Neow relic rewards are
  offered consecutively, using the same forkable extra-relic
  reward machinery as Lava Rock; a curse is appended to the
  deck **after** both pickup and any associated persistent
  deck-choice continuations complete. Native draws from all
  Neow-eligible relics except Bones; the restricted Silent
  emulator currently draws from the implemented pickup-safe
  subset (Booming Conch, Fishing Rod, Golden Pearl, Lava
  Rock, Nutritious Oyster, Stone Humidifier, Neow's Talisman,
  Pomander, Precise Scissors, New Leaf, Silver Crucible,
  Silken Tress). Injury is the available normal curse
  placeholder until a complete single-player curse pool and
  native RNG are ported. Cross-character and multiplayer
  rewards are excluded.
- **Scroll Boxes:** two bundles, each containing exactly
  two Commons and one Uncommon, with six distinct cards
  globally. Choosing a bundle adds all three persistent cards,
  preserving normal instance IDs and card-added hooks.
  The other bundle cannot be taken or skipped separately.
  The Silent prototype reward pool and RNG substitute for
  native unlocked-card creation; the Defect-only Claw
  exception is irrelevant in the single-player Silent target.

This custom-reward mechanism is *not* a complete native
multi-page event interpreter. It also does not yet handle
Neow's Bones' multiple Neow relic offers, Kaleidoscope's
cross-character card pools, or a complete native reward RNG.

## Silent-only scope and Lava Rock boss rewards

The current native-shaped generation profile is explicitly restricted to
**single-player Silent**. Cross-character card rewards are postponed at
the user's request; notably, **Kaleidoscope remains registered as a
native Neow identity but is excluded from the selectable opening pool**.
This is a deliberate prototype scope restriction, not a claim about
the actual native Kaleidoscope unlock condition. Multiplayer-only
Massive Scroll remains excluded separately.

**Lava Rock** now modifies only the first Act 1 boss reward generation:
in addition to the existing three-option boss relic choice, two extra
single-relic rewards are generated without replacement from unowned,
ordinary prototype relics. A persistent `HasTriggered` flag is set
on Lava Rock when those rewards are generated, and extra relic pickups
use the normal acquisition and deck-choice machinery. Separate
`ExtraRelicRewardIds` and `ExtraRelicsResolved` fields in
`RewardState` preserve independent pick decisions, deep-forking,
and the normal act-transition boundary.

The native relic queue, pool/RNG ordering, and any unimplemented
acquisition-specific hooks may still differ. No cross-character
reward mechanics are used in these new paths.

## Validation and remaining work

Tests check complete option registration, repeatability, three
distinct offers, the multiplayer-only Massive Scroll exclusion, curse
incompatibilities, conditional mutually exclusive pairs, coverage across
seeds, acquisition effects, Pomander/Precise Scissors/New Leaf deck choices,
and the Stone Humidifier rest-heal lifecycle.

This work advances native Act 1 *generation* without claiming complete
native Neow effects, map RNG call order, event page trees, or
shops/rewards/rest-site economy. Do not begin the requested final
fidelity test until the remaining generation gaps are addressed.
