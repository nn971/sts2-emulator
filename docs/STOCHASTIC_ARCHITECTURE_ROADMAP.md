Keep the four-Act Silent gameplay and active native inspector developments on separate tracks. ## S1 precise design

Implement a **read-only, outcome-blind** Chance Law query in `Sts2Emulator.Core` with:
- Semantic operation and call-site/owner/occurrence identifiers.
- Current legal **physical** outcome identities (distinct card instances) or stable intent IDs, eligibility and raw weights.
- Conditioning-policy ID and hidden **constraint** fingerprint; no selected native realization or RNG seed/cursor.
- Normalized/compact reference distribution, law version and `UNVERIFIED`/independently verified provenance.
- Immutable query result: the law cannot mutate RNG streams, card positions, game state or Nature's constraints.

`ReferenceNature` consumes that result, draws from it using an independent seeded test PRNG, returns one legal outcome and updates the Nature provider constraints through the existing selection/continuation mechanism. A known already-committed card produces **no Nature choice**. No native stream parity is required.

### Required independent tests

1. Weighted enemy intents after phase, previous-move, anti-repeat and formation eligibility filtering; candidate order changes must preserve stable identities and properly invalidate old support fingerprints.
2. Exhaustive **3–4 physical-card permutation** enumeration, including duplicate card models with distinct instances, known top/bottom positions, interleaved known and unknown slots, multiple draws, forced middle insertions, peek/scry and recycle/Perfect Fit restrictions.
3. Conditional joint probabilities after sequential decisions; compare exact small-state probabilities to independently enumerated reference permutations (not only Monte Carlo counts from ReferenceNature).
4. Proof-by-execution checks: Chance Law query must not change the state hash or depend on an already sampled realization when queried under the **same declared information state**; resolving a known card must never re-randomize it.
5. Invariant-based mechanical continuation: each chosen card triggers the real `DrawCards` / `CardDrawn` path once, with nested costs and interactions remaining engine-owned or explicitly unsupported.
6. Independent fork/save/load/replay, stale-event rejection, mode/provenance versions, state ownership and observation information-boundary tests.
7. Monte Carlo distribution smoke over many independently seeded episodes as a secondary check; a handful of rounds is **not** an evaluation of learned performance or general distribution accuracy.

## S2: training comparator and honest provenance

For #143 use two clear emulator policies:
- **ReferenceNature / declared-law control:** samples `p` at the semantic Nature sites without source cursor parity and delegates other chance to the engine's ordinary provider. This is the primary clear structural baseline.
- **Learned Nature:** selects `enemy_intent` and `draw_card` through `sts2-ai`; other stochastic operations go through the **identical** engine-owned provider.

Optionally check a **native-style commit-once control** (exact hidden deck realization using source-native RNG algorithms) against the ReferenceNature's specified conditional law. This is an additional distributional cross-check, not a requirement that counterfactual draws match native seed/cursor. Do not call an uncalibrated `p` the original-game conditional law.

Run matched source-state/seed sets; frozen player policy; fair observations/legal actions; independent full combat terminal outcomes; process-isolated 15-worker replay; report variance and multi-run comparisons. Keep toy/structural smoke distinct from performance evidence. `sts2-ai` owns optimizer, neural policy and experiments.

## S3–S5: migration without breaking the running project

- `SemanticDrawPileAdapterV111` now owns draw-pile membership/order correspondence checks and the shared semantic insert, remove, replace, shuffle, and physical-mutation boundary. Runtime draw-pile writes route through this adapter, which publishes the mechanics membership and provider state atomically. `CombatState.DrawPile` remains the mechanics membership source. `RunState` now persists the committed ReferenceNature cursor with versioned snapshot compatibility; the broader provider-state envelope and migration of all provider lifecycles remain open.
- Continue the adapter around `RunState`, `SemanticOtherChanceStateV111`, and named RNG bundles. Keep the single physical membership source and reject mismatches before publishing a state.
- Make the choice/continuation path consume semantic outcome identity, with a separate low-level native primitive journal. Preserve and version primitive-index scripts for compatibility.
- For each moved mechanic, add a native/scripted/ReferenceNature equivalence fixture and a legacy compatibility fixture **before** changing the default routing.
- Merge/rebase native inspector spectator/stream PRs independently on their current approved base. Carry forward source-specific hooks without binding them to Chance Law.
- Avoid touching the active Silent content branch while structural foundations are developed and tested on the isolated RNG integration branch.

## Deliverables, status discipline and ownership

- **S0:** ownership documents and a cross-linked site/state inventory are present; row-level coverage of every acceptance field remains open. This is partial inventory work, not a completed audit.
- **S1:** initial core Chance Law and simple ReferenceNature implementation is in `ChanceLawV111.cs`; the first independent exact small-state and seeded replay suite passes, while the complete acceptance matrix remains open.
- **S2:** the emulator has an opt-in seeded native-policy comparator with private stream state, replay traces and fail-closed provider guards, plus atomic semantic-combat, supported run-action and supported event-choice ReferenceNature JSONL sessions. The real Act 1 weak-combat source route now reaches its ordinary terminal reward through a narrowly allowlisted engine-owned reward stream. The learned-vs-reference A/B trainer and 15-worker report live in `sts2-ai`. Equivalence through one shared mechanics continuation remains part of S4.
- **S3–S4:** every runtime draw-pile mutation now goes through the adapter, which validates and returns mechanics membership and semantic provider order together. Native and controlled Nature turn-intent paths share both the pure eligibility selector and the same mechanics outcome-commit function. The semantic end-turn continuation now suspends, resolves, saves and replays automatic-pipeline target and enemy-action decisions with a chronological boundary transcript. `RunStochasticStateV111` groups run prototype RNG, native streams, the committed ReferenceNature cursor, and controlled run-action/event-choice continuations and histories; `CombatStochasticStateV111` groups combat native RNG and semantic deck state; `SemanticPilotStochasticStateV111` groups the control provider and stochastic provenance; `EventStochasticStateV111` groups event-context RNG and controlled random-choice continuation state. These owners keep legacy snapshot fields unchanged. Remaining semantic event state, the full mechanics/stochastic envelope, and unification of semantic and native-policy session routes remain migration work.
- **S5:** the player schema remains unchanged; versioned stochastic capabilities now negotiate supported Chance Law sites and ReferenceNature operations. `stochasticSessionCoverage` reports the semantic, run-action, and event-choice site sets by continuation schema. Semantic Nature pending payloads expose typed laws for intent/draw and the implemented target, automatic enemy-action index, generated-model, cost, transform, potion, random pile-card, pool-shuffle, draw-insertion, and shortlist events. Controlled event-effect/reward and run-action pending payloads expose generic index laws with request and boundary fingerprints; atomic run-action and event-choice sessions have separate schemas and return to ordinary run handles when committed. The existing JSONL exercise reached an intent law and a 10-card deferred draw law while confirming the fair player view omitted Chance Law data; this coverage addition has not been exercised. Broader cross-mode observations and capability coverage remain open.
- **S6:** ReferenceNature now handles live random-enemy target laws for effect, Sly autoplay, and autoplay sites in both the pilot and semantic end-turn continuations; bounded index laws for supported random enemy-action requests; uniform generated-card model laws at the two existing semantic generation sites; bounded laws for three semantic card-cost sites; a random hand-cost target selection; the random Power instance selection at the Jewelled Mask site; selected-card transform replacements; the current generated-potion model site; bounded Fisher–Yates steps for two semantic pool shuffles; bounded positions at two semantic draw-insertion sites; sequential conditional choices at three shortlist sites; supported run-action sampling; and supported controlled event-effect/reward sampling with intervening reward actions. Generic request-index Chance Laws remain `UNVERIFIED`. Offers, shops, formations, other generated-card and cost operations, and later map operations still need law descriptors and/or supported selection paths. Existing event/reward continuation support remains limited to its allowlisted sites; the new session does not establish their original source probabilities.
- **S7:** probability evidence/calibration; separately gated from #143.
- **F:** [inspector issue #122](https://github.com/nn971/sts2-emulator/issues/122) and [native plan](NATIVE_FIDELITY_INSPECTOR_PLAN.md).

**Success criterion for the unified architecture:** one mechanical execution path under Native, ReferenceNature, scripted replay and learned Nature; provider-private state and provenance are unambiguous; declared-law ReferenceNature passes independent conditional distribution tests; complete states replay/fork identically; player-visible observations remain fair. Source-exact RNG parity remains its own inspector acceptance gate.
